# Reset how your team meets.

## Three blocks

### Meeting Reset Workshop

AU$500 to 2,500: a live workshop covering the meeting audit, the Ten Questions, delegation training and a transcript-to-output demo.

### Meeting Waste Audit

AU$1,500 to 3,000, credited toward any program: your meeting load and cost calculated from your own calendar data, with a recommended Meeting Standard.

### Corporate transformation

AU$10,000 to 50,000 by scope: audit, Meeting Standard design, training, consent policy, Workspace rollout and a before-and-after measurement report.

## Line

Results are computed from your own numbers, never industry averages.
