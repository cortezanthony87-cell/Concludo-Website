# Paste a transcript. Leave with finished work.

## Intro

The Notewright Workspace turns any transcript into summaries, action plans, follow-up emails, decision logs and branded presentations in minutes. Capture on any device or platform, paste the text in, and the Workspace does the rest.

## Founding pricing

### Starter

AU$12 per month or AU$99 per year: core Workspace outputs, onboarding, responsible-use guidance, workbook chapter 1.

### Pro

AU$29 per month or AU$290 per year: all outputs including branded deck export, the full workbook, the education platform, monthly Q&A.

### Team

From AU$125 per month (AU$25 per seat, 5-seat minimum): shared workspace, admin controls, team playbooks, implementation support.

## Under the tiers

14-day free trial on Starter and Pro. Consent and responsible-use guidance ships in every tier.
