# About Concludo.

## Body

Concludo was built in Melbourne by Anthony Cortez, and it began the way the best businesses do, with a problem he lived every day.

For years, first working for himself and then across six years in Australian real estate, Anthony sat in more meetings than he could count. Appraisals, client calls, negotiations, planning sessions. Some were sharp. Most were not. Time and again he watched a room fill with good people, good intentions and real momentum, then watched all of it quietly leak away the moment everyone left. The conversation happened. The result did not.

He became convinced of two things. The first is that poorly run meetings drain more time, money and momentum from a business than almost anyone realises, not because people are careless, but because almost no one is ever taught how to run them. The second is more hopeful. The fix is teachable. Clearer purpose, fewer people, real ownership, recorded decisions and fast follow-up. Skills, not talent.

Concludo is the result. It teaches the method, and it provides the software that turns conversations into outcomes, so the value made in the room finally makes it out the door.
