## Positioning

Concludo is a meeting-productivity business: education plus software that turns any meeting transcript into finished work. This supersedes the earlier AI-pen education positioning, which is retained in `archive/` for reference only. The project traded as Notewright until 24 Aug 2026, when the company was registered as Concludo Pty Ltd and the brand renamed; Notewright-branded drafts are historical only and are not to be used going forward.

## Content rules

- Australian English.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Use commas, colons or "to".
- No statistics or percentages.
- No claims of certification, accreditation, partnership or endorsement.
- No device brand names.
- All calls to action point to the waitlist or email signup. Never to checkout.
- Pricing is founding-indicative and subject to revision before anything publishes.

## Brand

- Colours: primary `#16263F`, secondary `#21395C`, accent `#E2B53C`, dark accent `#BC8A1C`, light background `#F4F6FA`, body text near-black.
- Type: headings Poppins (bold for H1 and H2), body Inter.
- Buttons: navy `#16263F` fill, white text; hover or secondary gold `#E2B53C`.
