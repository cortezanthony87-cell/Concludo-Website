## Tone of Voice
- Commercially honest, evidence-led. Challenge overreach, vague positioning and fake certainty rather than indulge it.
- Clear and direct. Professional but approachable. Educational, not salesy.
- Australian English. No em dashes.
- Never imply live sales, validated pricing, payment processing, customer traction, formal accreditation, or public launch before those facts are true. Concludo is registered but pre-revenue.

## Key Messages
1. The value isn't in the device you capture on. It's in what you do with the conversation afterwards.
2. Concludo sells a method and a machine: a teachable method for running fewer, better meetings, and paste-first software that turns any transcript into finished work.
3. Capture on anything. Paste it in. Leave with finished work.

## Independence Statement
*Concludo is independent and capture agnostic. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any device maker or meeting platform.*

This statement must appear on every page that names a recorder, device brand or meeting platform (for example Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, an AI recorder or a smart pen). This replaces the old Notewright-era statement, which named one specific company; Concludo's rule is general and applies to any device maker or platform, not a single named one.

## CTA Rules
- All call-to-action buttons point to the waitlist/email signup only.
- No "Buy Now", "Add to Cart", or pricing with checkout.
- Never enter ABN, ACN or the registered office address on customer-facing content without explicit confirmation. Use "Melbourne, Australia" as the standing location reference.
