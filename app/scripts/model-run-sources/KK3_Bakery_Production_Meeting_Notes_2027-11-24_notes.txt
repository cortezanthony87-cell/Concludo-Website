Millstone and Ember Bakehouse
Weekly production meeting, Wednesday 24 November 2027, 1:30 pm, back office
Present: Roz Abernethy (head baker, chair), Dean Kowalski, Soo-jin Park, Anwar Siddiqui (dispatch), Felicity Mahuika (orders and office), Jarrah Thomson (apprentice)
Notes taken by Felicity

1. Christmas orders
Roz said the pre-orders for fruit mince pies and Christmas loaves are up on last year. Felicity's count stands at about 340 boxes, but she said it could be nearer 300 once the two cafes confirm. Roz wants the order book closed on Friday 10 December and no exceptions. Anwar asked what happens if a big order comes in on the 11th. Roz said she would rather say no than burn out the night crew, though she would reconsider if it came from Ridgeline Grocers.

2. Ridgeline Grocers
Anwar reminded the meeting that Ridgeline takes roughly 60 per cent of our loaves. Roz said that is far too much for one customer and that if they ever dropped the contract we would have to lay people off. Felicity has heard from a driver that Ridgeline might be talking to a bigger bakery in Melbourne, but nobody at the table could confirm it. Roz asked everyone not to repeat that outside the room. Felicity will ask the Ridgeline buyer for a catch up call in the first week of December. Roz asked the whole team to think about two other wholesale customers we could approach, and to bring ideas next week.

3. Flour supplier
The mill rang Roz with a new price of $1,180 a tonne. Roz said she had written $1,120 on the board last week and corrected it to $1,180 after the call. The mill will hold that price for twelve months, but only if we sign the new supply agreement by Friday this week. Roz said she is happy to sign. Soo-jin asked whether we should get a second quote from another mill. Roz said there is no time before Friday. Dean suggested signing a six month deal instead of twelve. After some discussion Roz said she would ask the mill for nine months as a middle ground and will sign if they agree. Roz to ring the mill tomorrow.

4. Night shift changes
The 2 am start team will be short from January. Roz decided that Dean will move to the 2 am start on Tuesday to Friday from the first week of January. Roz also decided that Jarrah will move to the 2 am start on Tuesday to Friday from the first week of February, once he has finished his block at college. Jarrah said he is a bit nervous about it and asked whether someone could be rostered with him in the first two weeks. Roz said she would put Soo-jin with him. Soo-jin said that was fine.

5. Sourdough price
At last month's meeting it was agreed to raise the large sourdough from $9.50 to $10.50 from December. Felicity said the cafe owners have been complaining. Roz said she is going to reverse that decision and keep the price at $9.50 until after Christmas. Dean said he thought it should have stayed at $10.50. Roz said she had heard him but the decision was made.

6. Equipment
Dean said the thermostat on oven 2 has been running a little hot, maybe by ten degrees or so. It has not caused any burnt batches yet. Roz said if it gets worse the oven would need to be shut down, and then we would be down to one deck oven in the busiest fortnight of the year. The service company can come on the 6th or the 8th of December. Nothing was decided about booking, because Roz wants to check the cost first.
Anwar and Felicity will look at the delivery routes together and work out whether the Saturday run can be done with one van. They will come back with a plan by Friday week.

7. Hygiene and allergens
Roz reminded everyone to wear the new hairnets and beard covers in the production area from Monday. The council inspector is expected some time before the end of January, though no date has been given. All staff must re-read the allergen sheet before their next shift.

8. Other
A customer wrote in to say the cinnamon scrolls were the best she had had in years. Roz said to pass that on to Soo-jin, who makes them. Soo-jin said it was Jarrah's mixing that fixed them. Dean asked whether the staff Christmas lunch is happening. Roz said she would think about it.
Felicity will send these notes to everyone in the team, including Jarrah's college.
Next meeting: Wednesday 1 December, 1:30 pm.
