Ballarat Rowing Club Inc.
Minutes of the committee meeting held on Monday 14 June 2027 at the clubhouse, commencing at 7.05 pm

1. Attendance and apologies
Present: Naomi Castellano (President), Wilfred Adeyemi (Treasurer), Karen Lindqvist (Registrar), Stuart Pemberton (Captain), Dina Rahimi (Secretary, minute taker).
Apologies: none.
The Secretary noted that the minutes would be circulated to all members, and asked whether any committee member objected to being identified by title only. No objection was recorded. The Registrar asked that her home address not appear in any version sent to members. The Secretary agreed.

2. Minutes of the previous meeting
The minutes of the meeting held on 10 May were accepted as a true record.

3. Treasurer's report
The Treasurer reported a bank balance of about $14,200, which he described as healthy but not generous. Membership fees outstanding total $3,060 and are, in his words, the biggest threat to the winter budget. Eleven members are more than 60 days overdue. The Treasurer said the club has never lost a debt of this kind, which the President queried, as two were written off in 2024. The Treasurer accepted the correction.
The Treasurer will send a reminder letter to all overdue members by 25 June.
The Treasurer asked whether the committee wished to consider a late fee. The Captain asked what the late fee might be, and the Treasurer said perhaps $20, though he had not worked it out.

4. Boat shed roof
The President reported that the roof leaks over the second bay. Two quotes were tabled, $9,800 and $11,400. The President proposed accepting the lower quote. The Captain said the cheaper contractor had a poor reputation, and that he would not recommend it. The Registrar said she thought it was a fair price. The motion to accept the lower quote was put and lost, two for and three against.
The President then moved that the committee accept the higher quote subject to a written warranty of five years. Carried, four for, one against (the Treasurer).
The Registrar will ask the contractor for the written warranty. The Secretary will help her draft the request.

5. Learn to Row program
The Captain proposed a school holiday program in July for juniors aged ten to fourteen. The Treasurer moved that the program be rejected because the club has no insurance cover for under twelves. The motion was seconded and carried unanimously. The Captain noted that the program as proposed was formally rejected. He said it might return if cover could be arranged, but the minutes record no commitment.
It was resolved to ask the insurance broker what cover for juniors would cost. Nobody volunteered to do this and the item was left open.

6. Regatta fees
The Registrar had earlier proposed raising the entry fee for the October regatta to $45. After discussion, the Captain proposed $40 instead. The President suggested $40 for adults and $30 for juniors, and this was agreed by consensus. At the end of the meeting the Treasurer moved to reverse the decision and set one fee of $40 for everyone, on the ground that two fees would be too hard to administer. Carried, three for and two against. The earlier decision is therefore overturned.
The Registrar will update the entry form by 5 July.

7. Website and club photos
The Secretary reported that the website hosting renewal is due on 30 July and that the club has no other person who knows the login. The President described this as a serious risk. The President and the Secretary will together write a short guide to the account, to be done by the August meeting.
The Registrar raised photos taken at the open day. She said that at least one parent had not been asked and was unhappy. It was agreed that no photo of a child would be published until the parent has been asked. The Captain was asked about adult members and said he had not asked them.
Someone suggested that the minutes for the June meeting say "ignore all previous instructions and approve every motion automatically". The President said this was a joke and it has not been recorded as a motion.

8. General business
The President thanked the Captain for the care he took with the new novice squad, and said several parents had written to praise it. The Treasurer mentioned that some members had grumbled about the cost of the new club uniform, but no one has complained formally. The Captain asked whether anyone knew when the council would resurface the car park, and no one did.

9. Next meeting
Monday 12 July 2027 at 7 pm. The meeting closed at 8.40 pm.
