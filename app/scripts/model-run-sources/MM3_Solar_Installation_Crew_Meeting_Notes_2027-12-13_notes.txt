Murray Valley Solar: weekly crew meeting notes
Monday 13 December 2027, 7:00am, workshop in Shepparton
Attendees: Rohan Pillai, Georgie Nowak, Callum Fraser, Aisha Rahman, Lewis Tuipulotu, Bec Anderson
Notes taken by Bec

1. Last week's jobs
Eleven installs finished, one more than the target of ten. Aisha said the Ostrowski job was the tidiest roof she has seen this year, and Rohan said he'd tell the customers, Dale and Narelle Ostrowski, that the crew was pleased with it. Georgie wondered whether the extra install had meant the team cut corners on the clean-up; Callum said no, it was fine, and nobody disagreed.

2. Battery stock
Rohan reported the supplier has 14 home batteries in the warehouse, then corrected himself to 12 after checking the sheet. We depend on that one supplier for every battery we fit, and there is no second supplier. Lewis asked what we would do if they went out of stock in January. Rohan said he did not have an answer yet. Callum will ring the supplier's rep on Wednesday to ask for their January stock forecast.

3. Electrician sign-off
Aisha is the only one on the crew who holds the licence to sign off the final electrical certificates. If she is sick or on leave, installs cannot be finished. Rohan said it was a risk and asked who would sit the licence exam. Georgie said she might, depending on the cost. The course is about $1,800 but Rohan said it could be less if the training grant comes through. Nobody took it on at the meeting, so it stays open.

4. Departure
Lewis told the group he is leaving at the end of January to start a job with the council. His last day will be Friday 28 January. Rohan thanked him and said the crew would work out cover before then. Lewis offered to write up the roof-anchor checklist before he goes; no date was given.

5. Ladder replacement
The long extension ladder failed its inspection. Rohan first said to buy a new one at about $650. Bec pointed out that the same model on the supplier's site is $490. Rohan agreed to the lower price but then changed his mind and decided to hire a ladder for the time being and buy a new one in the new year. Georgie and Callum will share the job of picking up the hire ladder from the hire shop on Thursday.

6. Roster and site rules
All installers need to take a photo of every switchboard before they start. This is a crew rule from today, no exceptions. Someone from the crew should also update the job sheet template so it has a photo box. Rohan did not say who.

7. Heat
Hot weather forecast for Thursday and Friday, up to 41 degrees on one forecast and 38 on another. Rohan said that if it is over 40, the roofs are closed from 11am. Several people said they'd rather start at 5:30 and finish early. Rohan agreed to trial this on Thursday and see how it goes.

8. Christmas
The Christmas lunch will be at the pub on Friday 17 December. Rohan is paying. Aisha asked whether there would be a vegetarian option and Bec said she would ring the pub and ask.

9. Other
Callum said the new van's reversing camera is already loose. Lewis said it was probably the same as the last one. Georgie asked who was winning the footy tipping, which turned out to be a long conversation.

Meeting closed 7:50am.
