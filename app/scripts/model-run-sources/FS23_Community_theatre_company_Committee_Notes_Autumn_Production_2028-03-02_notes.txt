Eastside Community Players, committee meeting notes
Thursday 2 March 2028, 7.30 pm, back room of the Mont Albert Scout Hall

Present: Rosalind Fenwick (president), Marcus Delaney (treasurer), Priti Anand (publicity), Colin Wakefield (director), Tess Abara (props and wardrobe), Glenys Harmer (secretary, taking notes)
Not present: Ivan Petrovic (stage manager, overseas until 20 March)

Started a bit late because the kettle was missing. Marcus found it in the kitchen cupboard under the tea towels.

1. Last meeting
Minutes accepted as read. Rosalind thanked Priti for the Facebook posts, which got more shares than anything last year. Lovely feedback from the audience about the Valentine's cabaret too.

2. Ticket prices for Blithe Spirit (May season)
Marcus proposed raising the full price ticket from $28 to $34. Colin thought that was too steep for a community show and wanted $30. Discussion went back and forth. A vote was taken: three for $32, two for $30, one abstention. Agreed at $32 full and $24 concession. Marcus says that should lift income by roughly twelve per cent, but that is his own estimate and he has not run the numbers properly.

3. Hall hire
Marcus said hall hire for the season is $2,400. Later he corrected this after looking at the invoice: it is $2,040. The corrected figure was accepted. Priti worried that the hall is the only venue available to us in May and if the council double books it again, as they did in 2026, there is nowhere else. Rosalind will ring the council to confirm the booking in writing. Nobody could say how long the council usually takes to reply.

4. Rehearsal schedule
Colin wanted to add Sunday afternoon rehearsals from 12 March. Tess said she could not manage Sundays. Colin withdrew the proposal and said he would try Tuesday evenings instead and report back next meeting. If the cast agree to Tuesdays, rehearsals could go from three a week to four.

5. Props and costumes
Tess has a long list. Glenys wondered aloud if she was taking on too much, given she is also doing the wardrobe with only one helper. Tess said she was fine but would welcome a second person. Priti and Glenys agreed to share the job of finding a volunteer for the wardrobe. No date was set. Someone needs to check whether the council hall's storage cupboard can hold the sofa from the set. Nobody put their hand up and it stays on the list.

6. Photos and publicity
Priti wants to take photos at the dress rehearsal for the Facebook page and the programme. She will ask each cast and crew member for permission before using their face or name. Two cast members are under eighteen, so Priti will also ask their parents. Ivan, who runs the backstage crew, has not been asked about his name and mobile number going on the poster as the contact for the technical queries. Nobody has asked him yet and he is away until the 20th. Rosalind said she would leave it until he is back rather than assume.

7. Treasurer
Marcus told the committee he plans to step down after the AGM in June. He did not want a long discussion. Rosalind said it would be hard to find someone who can do the grant acquittals as well. Marcus said he would write up a handover document but gave no date. Rosalind asked all committee members to think about who might take over and to bring a name to the next meeting.

8. Grant
The council arts grant of $3,000 is due to be lodged by Friday 31 March. This date is fixed. Rosalind will do the application and Marcus will supply the budget figures by Wednesday 15 March.

9. Other
Colin asked whether anyone had heard if the Box Hill group is doing the same play. Priti had heard a rumour that they were, but it was only a rumour. Open question, nobody knows.

Next meeting: Thursday 30 March, 7.30 pm, same place.

Meeting closed 9.10 pm. Supper was Glenys's lemon slice. Everyone agreed it was excellent.
