Wattle Gully Primary School P and C Association, meeting notes
Thursday 23 March 2028, school staff room, 7.00 pm
Notes by Katie Oduya (secretary)

Attendees: Jo Pellegrino (president), Samir Haddad (treasurer), Katie Oduya (secretary), Bronwyn Tait, Alan Forsythe, Pete Marchetti. Principal Helen Ng joined for item 1.

Tea and biscuits first. Pete brought the lamingtons again. Lots of chat about the school camp photos.

1. Principal's report
Helen said enrolments for next year look steady, about 310. She thanked the P and C for the new shade sail over the sandpit, which she said the Prep children use every day. She left at 7.20.

2. Photos for the newsletter
Katie asked whether she could take a group photo of the committee and put it in the newsletter and on the school Facebook page. Jo, Samir, Bronwyn and Pete said yes. Alan said he would rather not be in any photo or have his name published, so he is not to be named in the newsletter. Katie will crop him out of this one if he is in frame, and his name stays out of anything public.

3. School fete, Saturday 6 May
The date is fixed because the council permit is only valid for that day. Jo said we need 25 stall holders and so far have 14. Bronwyn thinks a few more will say yes once the flyer goes out. What if it rains? Jo said the hall can take about six stalls, but the rest will have to be cancelled, and nobody wanted to spend more on marquees. Decision on whether to hire marquees deferred to the next meeting.

4. Sausage sizzle
Pete is the only person who holds the food safety certificate and owns the gas barbecue trailer. Jo said we should not rely on one person. Samir suggested getting someone else certified. Nobody volunteered. Pete said he will be away the weekend before the fete but will be back by the Wednesday.

5. Budget for the fete
Samir said the fete budget is $3,200. Bronwyn pointed out that the figure on the spreadsheet is $2,300, so Samir corrected it and the budget is $2,300 with a note that the jumping castle is not yet included. Jumping castle quote is about $450, but Alan said he heard from another school that the company had put up their prices, so it may be more.

6. Raffle prizes
Jo asked for volunteers to collect donated prizes from local businesses. Nobody put a hand up. Jo will ask again by email.

7. Card reader
We need a card reader for the fete. Samir will set it up by 14 April. Jo mentioned Samir is new to the treasurer role and has not used the system before, so Bronwyn offered to sit with him in the first week of April. Samir said he would be glad of that.

8. Uniform shop
A vote was taken on opening the uniform shop one extra Friday each term. Moved by Alan, seconded by Bronwyn. Carried four to one, with Pete against because he said volunteers are already stretched. If the volunteers cannot be found, the extra Friday will not go ahead.

9. Next steps
Katie to send out the flyer to all families by Tuesday. Jo and Pete to look at the barbecue trailer gas bottles together. Bronwyn to ask the canteen about a thank you morning tea for volunteers. Next meeting Thursday 20 April.

Meeting closed 8.40 pm.
