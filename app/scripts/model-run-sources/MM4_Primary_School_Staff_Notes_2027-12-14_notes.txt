Wattle Grove Primary School, staff meeting notes
Tuesday 14 December 2027, 3:45pm, staffroom
Present: Helen Marchetti (principal), Dev Chaudhry (deputy), Fiona Lindqvist, Joe Nakamura, Abena Mensah
Notes by Dev

Opening and feedback
Helen opened by saying that several parents had told her at the pick-up gate how much their children enjoyed the garden day last week. She passed on particular thanks to Joe, who ran the seedling tables on his own for the whole afternoon. Helen added that she was still uneasy about the number of Prep children who have been away on Mondays this term, though she did not think it was a reason to do anything yet and wanted it kept in mind. Nobody had figures to hand.

Reading program for next year
The group discussed switching to the new phonics software from term one. Dev said the licence was about $2,400 a year, though the sales rep had mentioned it could be lower for a school of this size and he would need that confirmed in writing. Fiona said she was happy to try it, but admitted she finds new software a struggle and had needed a lot of help last time. Helen said she was a little worried Fiona would be left on her own with it, and asked who could sit with her in the first fortnight. Abena offered. Helen decided the school would adopt the software from term one and that Abena would sit with Fiona in the first two weeks. If the licence cost came back higher than $3,000, the matter would come back to the staff meeting. Dev will write to the rep this week to ask for the price in writing.

Assembly day
Helen proposed moving the weekly assembly from Thursday to Friday afternoon. Joe objected, as the Friday afternoon lesson is the only time he gets the whole of Year 3 for art. After some discussion Helen agreed to keep the assembly on Thursday as it is now. Later in the meeting Fiona raised the matter again, saying Thursday clashes with her pick-up arrangements. Helen said she would think about it over the holidays and decide in the new year.

Class allocations for next year
Joe will move to the Year 3 room and Abena will move to Year 4. Fiona will stay in Prep. Helen said the allocations would be sent to families in the last week of term. The group noted that Peter Kostas, who is on leave this week and was not at the meeting, was pencilled in to teach Year 5 and run the end of year camp. Nobody has asked him yet whether he would take on the camp. Helen said she would ring him when he is back, though she gave no date.

Camp
The camp has been booked for March but the bus company has not given a quote. Dev said it would be roughly $2,800, but that was only an estimate from last year. Helen asked whether anyone really thought parents would pay more than $400 per child, and nobody answered. Abena asked what would happen if fewer than 20 children signed up. Helen said, hypothetically, if numbers fell under 20 they would drop the second night, but she did not want to plan for it yet.

The end of year concert
It was suggested that the concert be held at the town hall this year. Helen asked Dev whether the hall was free on the last Wednesday of term, and Dev said he did not know. Dev will ring the hall tomorrow. Joe asked whether anyone had thought about where the parents would park, and Fiona said that was a question for the council.

Other
The photocopier is broken again. Helen said that the technician will come when he comes. Abena brought in cake for Fiona's birthday, which was a pleasant surprise.

Close
The meeting closed at 4:35pm. The next meeting is the first Tuesday of term one.
