Subject: Summary of Wednesday's visit to the Seymour and Broadford shops
From: Rosalind Achterberg, Volunteer Coordinator, Wattle Hill Community Op Shops
To: Graham Whitlock, Treasurer and Board Member
Date: Friday 3 March 2028

Dear Graham,

Thank you for suggesting that I go up to see the two northern shops before the board meets. I spent Wednesday afternoon with Joy Papadopoulos, who manages Seymour, Bill Tran, who runs Broadford, and Nadia Kassab, who coordinates the Saturday volunteers across both. Here is where we got to.

1. Opening hours
Seymour currently closes at 4 pm on weekdays and Broadford at 3 pm. Joy wanted both open until 5 pm to catch people after work. Bill said he cannot staff that with the volunteers he has. We settled on a compromise. Seymour will stay open until 5 pm on Thursdays and Fridays only, and Broadford will close at 4 pm on those two days. Everything else stays as it is. This starts on Monday 20 March.

2. Takings
Joy told me Seymour took about $6,800 last month. Nadia then pointed out that figure included a one off sale of donated furniture, and without it the number is closer to $5,900. I have used $5,900 in my notes. Bill said Broadford is "somewhere around four thousand", but he did not have the till reports with him, so please treat that as rough.

3. The van
Both shops depend on one donated van and one volunteer driver, Keith, who was not there on Wednesday. If Keith is unwell or away, no furniture gets collected at all. Nadia asked whether we could pay for a second driver's insurance cover. I do not know the cost. Can the board afford it? I have left that as a question for you. If the council approves our community parking permit for the van, Joy said Seymour would add a Wednesday collection round. That only happens if the permit comes through.

4. Broadford leadership
Bill told us he plans to step back at the end of April after six years. He has not told the other Broadford volunteers yet, so please keep this to the board for now. Nadia is unsure whether anyone is ready to take over. I am a little worried that Deepika, who has only been with us since November, would be asked to carry too much. I raised succession with Bill, but we did not decide anything.

5. Fire safety
Seymour's extinguisher tags look out of date. Someone needs to get both shops checked before the insurer's visit in May. Nobody took this on at the time, so it is still unassigned.

6. Racking decision deferred
Bill would like to replace the clothing racks at Broadford. Joy suggested we wait until after the end of financial year to see what is left in the budget. We agreed to put it off and look at it again in July.

7. Photos and names for the annual report
I took some photos of the volunteers at work on Wednesday. Before I use any of them in the annual report, could I ask whether you are comfortable with the volunteers' first names and photos going in? I have not asked the volunteers yet and would rather check with you first about how the board wants to handle it. I would also like to put Joy's and Bill's names in the thank you page.

What happens next
Joy will send me the extra roster for Thursday and Friday evenings by Friday 10 March. Nadia and I will work on the Saturday volunteer list together and have it ready by the end of March. I will price second driver cover and send you the figure by 17 March. The extinguisher check has no owner and no date yet.

Kind regards,

Rosalind Achterberg
Volunteer Coordinator
Wattle Hill Community Op Shops
