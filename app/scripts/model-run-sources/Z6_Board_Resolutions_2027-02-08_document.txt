TALLARONG DISTRICT FARMERS' CO-OPERATIVE LIMITED
MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Held on Monday 8 February 2027 at the Co-operative's boardroom, commencing at 6.30 pm

PRESENT: Margaret Oyelaran (Chair), Ian Fitzgerald, Svetlana Marchetti, Dev Patel, Colleen Barrie (Company Secretary)
APOLOGIES: Nil

1. OPENING AND RECORDING

The Chair opened the meeting at 6.30 pm. The Chair noted that the meeting would be minuted in writing only and that no audio recording would be made. All directors agreed to this.

2. CONFIRMATION OF PREVIOUS MINUTES

RESOLVED that the minutes of the meeting held on 11 January 2027 be confirmed as a true and correct record.
Moved: Ian Fitzgerald. Seconded: Svetlana Marchetti. Carried.

3. SILO UPGRADE

The Chair tabled a quotation of approximately $212,000 for upgrading Silo 3. Ian Fitzgerald moved that the Board approve the upgrade at that figure. Svetlana Marchetti moved an amendment that approval be conditional on the Co-operative obtaining two further written quotes. The amendment was accepted by the mover of the original motion. Dev Patel asked whether the existing silo could safely last another season. Ian Fitzgerald said that the silo "has never had a safety failure", but Svetlana Marchetti noted that the last engineering inspection was more than three years ago.

RESOLVED that the Board approve the upgrade of Silo 3 at a cost not exceeding $220,000, subject to the Co-operative obtaining two further written quotes before any contract is signed.
Moved: Ian Fitzgerald. Seconded: Svetlana Marchetti. Carried, three votes to one, Director Patel voting against.

Director Patel asked that his dissent be recorded but that the reasons for it not be circulated outside the Board. The Chair agreed.

The Chair directed that the Chief Executive Officer is not to sign any contract for the upgrade until the further quotes have been presented to the Board.

4. SIGNING AUTHORITY

The Company Secretary reported that, at present, the Chief Executive Officer is the only person authorised to approve payments above $10,000. The Board noted that this is a single point of failure, particularly as the Chief Executive Officer intends to retire at the end of September 2027.

RESOLVED that a second authorised signatory be appointed for payments above $10,000.
Moved: Margaret Oyelaran. Seconded: Colleen Barrie. Carried unanimously.

Colleen Barrie and Svetlana Marchetti will jointly prepare a draft delegation of authority policy for the March meeting.

5. MEMBER FEE INCREASE

Ian Fitzgerald moved that annual member fees be increased by ten per cent from 1 July 2027. Dev Patel said he believed that most members would accept an increase, but he had not seen any figures.

RESOLVED that annual member fees be increased by ten per cent from 1 July 2027.
Moved: Ian Fitzgerald. Seconded: Dev Patel. Lost, one vote for and three against.

The Board then discussed whether a smaller increase might be put to a future meeting. If a five per cent increase were proposed, the Chair said it would need member consultation first. No resolution was made.

6. OVERDUE ACCOUNTS

The Company Secretary reported that two large member accounts, together owing approximately $38,400, are now more than ninety days overdue, and that one of the two, which accounts for most of the debt, represents the Co-operative's largest single customer. Who will contact the largest customer was not settled. No director offered to do so.

7. SUCCESSION

The Chair asked how the Board will recruit the next Chief Executive Officer and whether it should use a recruitment firm. This item was not resolved.

RESOLVED that discussion of the Chief Executive Officer succession plan be adjourned to the meeting of 8 March 2027.
Moved: Margaret Oyelaran. Seconded: Ian Fitzgerald. Carried.

8. GENERAL BUSINESS

The Chair thanked Svetlana Marchetti for her work on the annual field day, which was widely praised by members. Ian Fitzgerald asked whether any director had concerns about sharing these minutes with the member newsletter. Directors agreed that the minutes may be shared, but only after the Company Secretary has removed item 3's dissent notation.

9. NEXT MEETING AND CLOSE

The next meeting will be held on Monday 8 March 2027. There being no further business, the Chair closed the meeting at 8.05 pm.

Confirmed as a true and correct record:

Margaret Oyelaran, Chair
