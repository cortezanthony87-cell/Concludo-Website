Bluegum Ground Handling, Terminal 2 ramp crew
Weekly crew meeting notes, Friday 10 March 2028, crew room, 5.30 am

Attendees: Shane Pritchard (shift manager), Lakshmi Naidoo (ramp supervisor), Dario Costa (loader), Fiona Mbeki (baggage hall), Kyle Hendry (pushback driver), Tess Warrick (loader)

1. Last week
Ninety four turnarounds, three delayed. Two were weather. The third was the Perth flight where the belt loader would not start and Dario had to hand load. Shane said well done to Dario and Tess, nobody was hurt and the aircraft left only twelve minutes late.

2. Pushback tug
Kyle is the only person on the shift who holds the current tug licence. Shane said that if Kyle is sick or on leave we cannot push anything back and would have to borrow a driver from the other terminal, who may not be free. Shane called it a single point of failure and said it has to be fixed. Lakshmi suggested training two more people. Dario said he would do the course. Tess said maybe, depends on the roster. Shane to book the course, no date yet.

3. Roster swap
Fiona asked to swap her Sunday 19 March early shift with Tess. Tess said she would do it only if she gets the following Saturday off. Shane said he would think about it. Not decided. Lakshmi to let Fiona know Monday.

4. Safety audit
The external safety audit is on 24 March and the date is fixed. Every crew member needs the fatigue module and the dangerous goods refresher completed before then. Shane said anyone who has not done these by 20 March will be taken off the ramp. Lakshmi will print a list of who has done what. Shane said he is a bit worried about Tess finishing the dangerous goods module because she has failed the quiz twice and finds the written side hard. Lakshmi will sit with her and Dario offered to help.

5. Photos and the noticeboard
Shane asked if he could take a crew photo on his phone for the safety poster in the crew room, with names under it. Dario, Fiona, Lakshmi and Tess said yes. Kyle said no, he would rather not be in it and does not want his name on the board either. Shane said that was fine, the poster will go ahead without Kyle. Nobody else raised an objection.

6. Casual rate and contract rumour
Shane said the new hourly rate for casuals will be $41.50. Lakshmi pointed out that the enterprise agreement figure is $43.20, so the $41.50 was wrong. Corrected to $43.20 from 1 April. Shane said he has heard the airline might change handling contractors next year but it is only a rumour. If it is true, about half of the work on this terminal would go. Nobody knows more.

7. Cleaning of the crew room
The fridge needs cleaning out. Somebody needs to do it. Nobody volunteered.

8. Other
