Title: Ridgeback Hill Estate, vintage planning meeting
Date: Monday 27 March 2028
Format: in person, cellar door tasting room, five participants, transcript

Fiona McAllister: Right, everyone grab a coffee. Sorry about the noise, the dishwasher is on. We've got a lot to get through and Rohan has to be back in the winery by eleven.
