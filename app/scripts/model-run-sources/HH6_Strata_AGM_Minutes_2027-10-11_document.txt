OWNERS CORPORATION PLAN PS 840217 (Jacaranda Court, 28 Mill Street, Richmond)
MINUTES OF THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
Held on Monday 11 October 2027 at 6:30 pm in the ground floor common room

1. Attendance and quorum

Present in person: Gwendolyn Osei (Lot 3, Chairperson), Ravi Menon (Lot 7, Secretary), Alistair Boyd (Lot 9, Treasurer), Yuki Tanabe (Lot 1), Tom Brennan (Lot 5) and Patrick Dwyer (Lot 11). Also present: Dominic Sayers of Kestrel Strata Management (the manager), who took the minutes.

Proxies: Ines Carvalho (Lot 2) gave her proxy to Gwendolyn Osei. The owner of Lot 6 gave a proxy to Ravi Menon. The owner of Lot 10 gave a proxy to the manager. The proxy for Lot 4 was received but was not signed and so was not accepted.

Quorum check: twelve lots are entitled to vote. Six owners were present and three valid proxies were held, so nine lots were represented. The rules require half of the lots, which is six. The Chairperson declared a quorum and the meeting opened at 6:34 pm.

The Chairperson asked whether anyone objected to the meeting being recorded to help with the minutes. Mr Brennan said he did not wish to be recorded. The recording was therefore not made, and the minutes are from the manager's handwritten notes. Ms Tanabe said she was happy to have her name appear in the minutes. The owners of Lots 6 and 10 were not asked, as they were not present.

2. Minutes of the last meeting

The minutes of the previous AGM were taken as read. Mr Dwyer noted that they recorded a carried-over item, the replacement of the intercom, which is dealt with under item 7.

3. Financial report

Mr Boyd tabled the accounts. The administrative fund spent about eighty-six thousand dollars for the year against a budget of about eighty thousand dollars. The overrun was mostly water and the lift service. He first stated the sinking fund balance as one hundred and twelve thousand dollars. Later in the meeting, after checking the bank statement, he corrected this: the correct balance is one hundred and twenty-one thousand dollars. The earlier figure had transposed two digits. The manager recorded the corrected figure.

Mr Boyd said he thought the fund is probably adequate for the next five years, but that this depends on the lift. Mr Brennan said that the fund has always been enough and always will be. Mr Boyd said he would not put it that strongly.

Motion 1: That the financial statements for the year be received. Moved Mr Boyd, seconded Ms Tanabe. Carried unanimously.

4. Overdue levies

The manager reported that the owner of Lot 8 has not paid levies for two quarters and owes about four thousand two hundred dollars. The manager said a final notice has been sent. Mr Dwyer asked whether the corporation would take the matter to the tribunal if payment is not received. The Chairperson said that this is a question for the committee. The manager is to send a second reminder letter to Lot 8 within seven days.

5. Insurance renewal

The insurance renewal is due on 30 November. This is a fixed date and the policy cannot lapse. The manager will obtain three quotes. Ms Osei said the premium last year was about nineteen thousand dollars and that she expects an increase of ten percent or more, but she could not be sure.

Motion 2: That the committee be authorised to accept the cheapest comparable insurance quote without a further general meeting. Moved Mr Menon, seconded Mr Boyd. Carried, seven votes for, two against, by a show of hands and proxies.

6. Lift maintenance and the single contractor

The lift is serviced by one contractor, and nobody else knows its controls. Mr Menon said that if that contractor went out of business, the corporation would have no alternative and the lift could be out of service for weeks. He is concerned that this is a single point of failure. Ms Tanabe agreed with this. Mr Brennan and Mr Dwyer also agreed. Mr Boyd said it is a risk, but that finding a second contractor would take time. The committee will ask the manager to approach one other lift contractor for a quote.

Motion 3: That the lift contract be put out to tender this year. Moved Mr Menon, seconded Ms Tanabe. After discussion, Mr Boyd asked that the motion be varied so the tender is limited to seeking one additional quote, not a full tender, because of the cost. Mr Menon agreed to the change as a compromise. The varied motion, to obtain one additional quote, was carried, six votes for, three against.

7. Carried over from last year: the intercom

The intercom system was agreed in principle last year, but the quote of about eleven thousand dollars has lapsed. Mr Dwyer said the intercom is "constantly broken". Ms Tanabe said it works most of the time.

Motion 4: That the intercom be replaced at a cost not exceeding twelve thousand dollars from the sinking fund. Moved Mr Dwyer, seconded Ms Tanabe. Lost, three votes for, six against.

Mr Dwyer then moved that the intercom item be deferred to the next general meeting while a new quote is obtained. Carried unanimously.

8. Garden and fence

Motion 5: That the front fence be repainted this year. Moved Mr Brennan. Before the vote, Mr Brennan withdrew the motion, saying the cost is not justified at present. No vote was taken.

The painters engaged for the stairwell are to keep the car park clear while they work, and the gardeners are to cut back the jacaranda away from the entrance by the end of the month. No person was assigned to follow up either instruction, and the manager said he would assume the strata office would deal with them.

9. Pets and noise

An owner had asked in writing whether the corporation would consider allowing small pets. Ms Osei said that, hypothetically, the rules could be changed by special resolution, but that it would need three quarters of the lots. The owners present did not wish to proceed. No motion was put.

Mr Dwyer asked who would clean the stairwell windows. No one answered, and the question was moved on.

Motion 6: That the earlier decision to reduce the number of committee members from five to three be reversed. Moved Mr Menon, seconded Mr Boyd. This was a reconsideration of a motion carried last year. Carried, eight votes for, one against. The committee remains at five members.

10. Election of the committee

The following were elected unopposed: Ms Osei, Mr Menon, Mr Boyd, Ms Tanabe and Mr Dwyer. Mr Brennan said he did not wish to stand. Ms Osei thanked Mr Brennan for his work over the past three years, and the room applauded.

11. Other business

Ms Tanabe asked whether the winter heating of the common room would be funded this year. The Chairperson said it was a good question and nobody had an answer. Mr Brennan said that the building has never had a major problem with heating and never will.

Ms Osei said the committee thanked the manager for the clear paperwork this year. Mr Boyd added that the pack was the best he has seen.

Before closing, Mr Dwyer said that it was a pity so few owners come to these meetings. Ms Tanabe said the weather was perhaps a factor. Several owners talked about the football for a few minutes.

The meeting closed at 8:12 pm.

Confirmed as a true record:
Gwendolyn Osei, Chairperson
