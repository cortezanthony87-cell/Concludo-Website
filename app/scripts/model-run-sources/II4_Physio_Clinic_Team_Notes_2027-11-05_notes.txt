Wren Street Physiotherapy, team meeting, Friday 5 November 2027
Present: Naomi Fitzgerald, Rashid Karimi, Teodora Vasile, Bridget Maloney

Opening and recording
Naomi asked at the start whether she could record the meeting on her phone to help her write these notes. Teodora and Bridget said that was fine. Rashid said he would prefer not to be recorded, so Naomi did not record and these notes were written by hand as the meeting went on. Rashid said he was happy for the notes themselves to be kept in the shared folder.

Waiting times
Naomi reported that new patients are waiting about three weeks for a first appointment, and closer to four weeks on Rashid's list, though she said the figures come from the booking system and may be a bit out. Rashid said that with several of his long-term patients finishing their treatment he expects the wait to shorten over the next month, but he did not want that written down as a promise. Naomi asked whether the clinic should accept fewer new referrals until the wait comes down. Teodora argued against it. Nothing was decided, and Naomi said she would raise it again in the new year.

Thursday late clinic
Last month the team agreed to move the late clinic from Thursday to Wednesday evenings. Bridget showed the team the roster, which would leave nobody on reception on Wednesdays after four, because her hours are fixed by childcare. After some discussion Naomi reversed the decision: the late clinic stays on Thursdays. Bridget thanked everyone for reading the roster. Nobody else objected.

Weekend clinic
Rashid had floated the idea of a Saturday morning clinic. Within a few minutes he withdrew it, saying that he did not have the energy for six days and that the staffing would be too thin to be worth the trouble. Naomi thanked him for raising it.

Shockwave machine
Teodora is the only person at the clinic trained to use the shockwave machine. Naomi called that a single point of failure, because if Teodora is away or leaves, the machine sits idle and patients booked for it have to be cancelled. If the team trained a second person, they would also need a second licence. Naomi asked Rashid whether he'd be interested in the course, and he said maybe. The course costs roughly eight hundred dollars, though Naomi said it might be nearer one thousand once travel is included. The course date is not yet known. Rashid will look at the course and let Naomi know. Teodora and Naomi will also put together a list of the patients booked for the shockwave machine over the next month, so there is a plan if Teodora is ill.

Treatment table
The clinic needs a new electric treatment table for room two. Naomi said the supplier has quoted twenty-eight hundred dollars, but she would like a second quote before deciding. The purchase is deferred until the second quote arrives, and Naomi will chase that quote by next Wednesday.

Aged care contract
Naomi reminded everyone that the aged care facility contract brings in about a third of the clinic's income, and it is the only contract of its kind. If the facility changed provider, the clinic would feel it immediately. She said the contract is up for renewal in the new year and the facility's manager has been positive so far. The team agreed it is not in a position to be complacent.

Provider audit
The health fund provider audit takes place on Wednesday 24 November and cannot be moved. Bridget said the paperwork is mostly ready, but she does not yet know whether the auditor wants paper or electronic records. Naomi said that should be clarified with the fund, and Bridget rang the fund during the afternoon tea break and reported back that electronic records are fine. She will confirm the audit time with the fund's office.

Neighbouring clinic
Teodora mentioned that a patient told her the clinic two streets away is closing in the new year. Naomi said that she had not heard it from anywhere else and that the team should not act on it.

Cancellations
Naomi asked that reception offer any same-day cancellation slots to the waiting list before the end of each day. Bridget said she already does when she has time. Who will book the room for the January planning day was raised and left open.

Feedback and a concern
Naomi read out two patient reviews from this week, both praising Teodora's explanations and the way she followed up. Teodora said thank you and went slightly pink. Separately, Rashid said quietly after the meeting closed that he thinks Bridget is stretched thin and has not said so. Bridget had left the room by then, and he asked that it not be raised with her directly yet.
