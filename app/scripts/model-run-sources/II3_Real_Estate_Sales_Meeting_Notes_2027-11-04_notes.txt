Weekly sales meeting, Thursday 4 November 2027
Attendees: Dina Aboud (sales manager), Kofi Mensah, Leanne Whitcombe, Anders Holm, Tess Brannigan
Notes taken by Tess.

1. Pipeline
Dina opened by saying the month has been steady but not spectacular. Appraisals booked this month are about forty, possibly forty-five depending on who you ask; Tess is still reconciling the diary against the booking sheet. Kofi has two vendors who are close to signing and one who has gone quiet. Leanne said her Saturday open at Wattle Court drew a big crowd and several people asked about price guides. Kofi said the crowd was the best he'd seen all spring.

2. 14 Wattle Court
The vendors have been told to expect somewhere between nine hundred and a million dollars, but they are hoping for more. Dina said that if it clears one point one million, the agency would reconsider the way it prices similar homes in the street. No one thought that was likely. Open question from Leanne: does the vendor want an auction or a private sale? Kofi said he'd ask them, and later in the meeting he confirmed the vendors had told him they prefer a private sale, so that is settled.

3. Commission split on shared listings
This was the longest discussion. Anders wanted a sixty/forty split in favour of the listing agent. Leanne wanted fifty/fifty, arguing that the selling agent does most of the hard work. After about ten minutes the group settled on fifty-five/forty-five in favour of the listing agent, as a compromise. Dina will write it into the sales policy and it applies from December.

4. Competitor news
Kofi heard from a vendor that Harlow Realty is dropping its fee to one point six per cent. Nobody could confirm this, and Dina said it should be treated as hearsay until someone sees a written fee schedule. Tess asked whether anyone is meant to follow up on that. Nobody volunteered.

5. Signage
Tess raised the cost of the new sign boards. A vote was taken on whether to replace all the old boards at once. Dina, Kofi and Tess voted in favour; Leanne and Anders against. Carried three to two. Tess will order them. A date for the order was not discussed.

6. Photography
Anders suggested the agency use a new photographer for the Wattle Court listing instead of the usual one. The suggestion was withdrawn after Dina pointed out that the vendor already approved the current photographer's quote. Someone has to book the photographer, but nobody picked it up.

7. Open home follow-up
Last weekend's open homes generated a long list of attendees who have not been called. Kofi and Leanne will share the calling between them and finish by Monday. Dina asked that they log every call in the system, whoever makes it.

8. Consent and sharing these notes
Dina said head office would like a copy of the notes every month and asked whether everyone was happy for the notes to go with names attached. Four people said yes. Anders said he would rather his comments on the commission split were not attributed to him in anything that leaves the building, and the others were fine with that. Dina agreed to write "a team member" for those comments in the version sent to head office. The version kept internally will use names.

9. Training
Dina reminded the team that the compliance refresher is due and she wants everyone through it before the end of the month. The whole team needs to book a session.

10. Other business
Leanne said the new office coffee machine is excellent. Brief small talk about footy tipping and the weather for Saturday's opens.

Praise: Dina thanked Kofi for the way he handled a very difficult vendor last week and said the client had written to say how reassuring he was.

Concern: Tess said, quietly, that she is a little worried about the volume of paperwork now that the team is busier and she is still the only person who files the contracts. She did not ask for anything to be done.
