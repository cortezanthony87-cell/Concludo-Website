COACHING SESSION NOTES
Coach: Danielle Oyelaran
Client: Elspeth Marchetti (owner, Marchetti and Daughters Bakery, Bendigo)
Also present: Ravi Bhandari (production lead), for the second half only
Session 4 of 8. Held Thursday 14 January 2027, 9.00 to 10.30am at the bakery.

Before we started
Elspeth said she is fine with me keeping notes and sharing a copy with Ravi. She was also fine with me using a de-identified version in my own supervision. Ravi arrived at 9.45 and I told him then what we had been doing. He said no problem.

My reflections on the first half
Elspeth arrived tired. She told me she had been up at 3am for the third day running because the second oven tripped again. I noticed that every time she described a problem she described it as hers alone, even when it plainly involved other people. I think she is carrying the bakery's worries as a matter of identity. That is an observation, not a diagnosis, and I will test it with her next time rather than assume it is right.

She was warm and funny when she talked about the Christmas trade. By her account it was the best December in the shop's history, which I could not check, and she said "we never get complaints." I will gently challenge the word "never" later, because she mentioned a complaint about a late wedding cake in the same session.

What she wants in the next twelve months: to stop working Saturdays, to bring Ravi up to a proper operations role, and to consider a second shop in Eaglehawk, though she kept saying she is "not ready to talk about that yet." We agreed to leave Eaglehawk alone today.

Agreed in the first half
1. Elspeth will hire a Saturday supervisor rather than hoping she can step back. She said she would advertise this week.
2. She will stop answering supplier calls after 2pm. Ravi will take them instead. (Ravi had not yet joined at that point, so this is subject to him agreeing. See below.)
3. She will book an electrician to look at the oven circuit. She said she would do it "as soon as I can". No date.

Second half, with Ravi
Ravi agreed to take the supplier calls after 2pm on weekdays. He asked for a small allowance in his hours and Elspeth said they would sort it out, but neither of them suggested a date or an amount. I flagged this and they agreed to talk it through privately and not decide in the session.

Ravi raised a worry that he did not want to put in front of Elspeth before: that two of the younger bakers have said they will leave if the 4am start does not move. Elspeth said she had not known. She was quiet for a while and then said she would think about it. I asked what a later start would cost her. She guessed "maybe twenty percent on the early trade," but she said she had no figures to back it up, only a feeling. They decided not to change the start time yet. They will look at it again at the next session.

I asked a hypothetical: if the oven had failed on a Saturday in December, what would have happened? Ravi said they would have lost the whole day. Elspeth said she would have called her brother in Melbourne. I asked whether that was a plan. She laughed and said no.

Ravi gave Elspeth some positive feedback, unprompted. He said her decision to keep prices steady through winter made the shop busier and he thought it was brave. She seemed pleased and slightly embarrassed.

Who will do what
Elspeth: advertise the Saturday supervisor role this week. Book the electrician, no date given.
Ravi: take supplier calls after 2pm from Monday 25 January, subject to the allowance.
Danielle: send Elspeth the one page on delegation I mentioned, by Monday.
Nobody: review the roster for the weekend bakers. Elspeth said "someone should" and Ravi said he was already full. I did not push.

For next session (4 February)
- Come back to the 4am start, with whatever figures Ravi can pull together.
- Ask Elspeth about the word "never".
- Eaglehawk stays off the agenda unless she raises it.
