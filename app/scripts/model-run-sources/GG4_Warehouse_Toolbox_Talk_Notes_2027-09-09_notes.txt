TOOLBOX TALK NOTES
Site: Altona distribution warehouse, Bay 3 briefing area
Date: Thursday 9 September 2027
Run by: Craig Nowak, shift supervisor
Duration: about fifteen minutes, start of the morning shift

TOPIC 1: Forklift near misses
