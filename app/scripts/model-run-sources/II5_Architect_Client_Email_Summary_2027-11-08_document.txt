Subject: Callum and Bronwyn Takahashi, where we landed on Friday (Stanhope Street extension)

Hi Callum and Bronwyn,

Thanks for coming in on Friday, and for the biscuits. I hope the footy was worth the early start on Saturday. Here is where I think we got to. Please read it through and tell me if I have got anything wrong.

1. Kitchen layout. We went round in circles on the island. Callum wanted it to seat four, Bronwyn wanted more floor space. In the end you both agreed on a smaller island with two stools and a deeper bench along the wall, which I will draw up. I think that is a good compromise.

2. Rooftop deck. Callum raised the idea of a deck over the new rear room. After talking about the cost and the extra structure it would need, Callum withdrew the idea. I have taken it off the drawings.

3. Solar panels. You decided to put the solar decision off until after the extension is built, when you will know how much roof is left. I will leave a space for the conduit so the option stays open.

4. Windows. The large corner window is a custom order, and as far as I know only one fabricator in Melbourne makes frames to that size. The quote I have says about fourteen weeks, but they have been slipping lately and it could be longer. If they slip, the whole timeline slips. I would like to place the order as soon as the plans are approved.

5. Budget. I said on Friday that the construction budget we are working to is six hundred and forty thousand dollars. That was wrong. After checking my notes, the figure you gave me was six hundred and sixty thousand dollars. Please use six hundred and sixty. That also includes a ten per cent allowance for things that go wrong, which I would not touch.

6. Council. The application has to go in by Friday 26 November. After that the council's end-of-year closure means nothing is looked at until late January, so there is no room to slip. I will have the drawings ready, but I need two things from you first.

7. What I need from you.
(a) Callum, please send me the photos of the back fence and the neighbouring windows by Wednesday.
(b) Callum and Bronwyn, please choose the bench stone and the floor tiles together. I need your choice by Friday 12 November. I know that is quick. Is that fair, or is it too much, given you are both working? I will leave that with you.
(c) I will send you the revised plans by Friday 12 November as well.

8. Neighbours. The builder mentioned that the owner at number twelve might object to the extension because of the shadow over the side garden. He heard this from someone at the hardware shop, so I would treat it as gossip. Still, if the neighbour did object, we would probably have to cut the height of the new wall, and that would affect the ceiling in the main bedroom.

9. Soil test. We still need one before the engineer can finish the footings. I am not sure whether you or I should book it. I will not assume. Let me know which you prefer.

10. Site access. Whoever does the demolition should be told to protect the existing jacaranda, which Bronwyn is attached to. I will include that in the tender documents.

A word on the heritage question. If the overlay does apply to your street, it could add three to four months. I have asked the council whether it applies, but they have not yet answered. I should know by next week.

On a different note, Bronwyn, you asked on Friday whether the extension would make the house darker. Honestly, with that corner window, who would ever say yes? I am confident it will be lighter.

One more thing. Bronwyn, you mentioned in passing that your mother is staying with you over the summer. I did not want to make a fuss, but a building site is noisy and dusty, and I would not want it to be hard on her. It does not change anything yet, but let me know if you want me to adjust the staging.

You were both generous with your time on Friday and made it a pleasure. Many clients cannot say what they want. You could, and that has made the drawings easier.

Warm regards,
Nadia Papadakis
Architect, Fernhill Studio
