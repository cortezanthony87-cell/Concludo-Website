CRUSTWORTH WHOLESALE BAKERY
Minutes of the production planning meeting
Thursday 16 March 2028, 1.00 pm, upstairs office, Footscray

Present: Antonia Ferrante (managing director, chair), Hamid Rostami (head baker), Josephine Cheung (sales and accounts), Callum Dwyer (delivery coordinator), Priti Nambiar (quality and food safety)
Apologies: none

1. Opening
Antonia opened the meeting and noted that the Easter trade would be the biggest of the year. Hamid said the new proving cabinets have made a visible difference to the sourdough, and Josephine passed on a compliment from the Brunswick cafe group, who described the last order as the best loaf they had tasted from any supplier. The team was pleased.

2. Easter volumes
Josephine reported that hot cross bun orders are up on last year. She first gave the total as 9,000 dozen, then corrected it to 6,900 dozen after rechecking the order sheet, which had a duplicated customer. Hamid said he believes the ovens can handle about 7,500 dozen across the week but has not tested it. If the extra orders from the Sunshine supermarket stores are confirmed, the total could reach 8,000 dozen, and Hamid said in that case the Thursday night shift would need an additional baker. Antonia said she would approve the extra baker only once the supermarket confirms in writing.

3. The supermarket contract
Antonia reminded the group that the Sunshine supermarket group accounts for roughly half of all revenue. The contract renews on 30 June. She said losing it would be very hard to recover from. Josephine heard from a delivery driver that the supermarket is talking to a larger bakery in Altona, but that is hearsay and she has no evidence. Antonia asked Josephine to arrange a meeting with the supermarket buyer before the end of April. Antonia added that if they ask for a lower price, she would consider up to three per cent but nothing more.

4. Flour supply
All flour is bought from a single mill in the Wimmera. The miller has said there may be a price rise from July, perhaps around eight per cent, though nothing has been announced. Hamid suggested a hypothetical, that if the mill had a breakdown during Easter week, the bakery would run out within four days. Priti and Hamid will jointly get quotes from two other mills and report back by Friday 31 March.

5. Delivery vans
Callum said van 3 failed its roadworthy check and will be off the road for about two weeks. He proposed hiring a van for the Easter period at roughly $380 a day. Antonia agreed to the hire. Callum was asked whether he could manage the extra Good Friday deliveries, given that he has covered for the other driver since February. Callum said he could, though Josephine noted that he looked tired and asked that someone keep an eye on his hours. Antonia said she would do that herself, and would look at the roster next week.

6. Food safety
Priti reported that the allergen labelling audit is due on 12 April and that the sesame label on the seeded loaves still needs reprinting. It was agreed that all production staff must check labels against the allergen sheet at the start of every shift. Priti will brief the night team on Monday.

7. Equipment
The old dough divider has stopped working twice this month. Hamid asked if a replacement could be budgeted this year. Antonia said she did not know and would need to see a quote. No one has yet agreed to obtain it. It was left on the list.

8. Other business
Priti asked whether the staff car park could be repainted before the audit. Antonia said that is a question for the landlord. No further business.

Actions
Josephine: book the meeting with the supermarket buyer, before the end of April.
Priti and Hamid: two flour mill quotes, by Friday 31 March.
Priti: brief the night team on allergen checks, Monday.
Antonia: review the delivery roster, next week.
Callum: arrange the hire van.
Dough divider quote: no owner.

The meeting closed at 2.10 pm.

Minutes prepared by Josephine Cheung and checked by Antonia Ferrante.
