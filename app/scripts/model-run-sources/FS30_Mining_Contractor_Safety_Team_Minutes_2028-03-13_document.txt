RED RIDGE DRILLING SERVICES
Minutes of the safety team meeting
Monday 13 March 2028, Karratha office, 7.30 am

Present: Josh Calloway (safety manager, chair), Maree Dunstan (health and safety representative), Tariq Hosseini (day shift supervisor), Lina Voss (training coordinator), Ben Okafor (site coordinator)
Apologies: none

1. Previous minutes
Taken as read. Lina noted the heat cards ordered last month have still not arrived.

2. Near miss reporting
Josh reported 14 near misses for the quarter. Maree said that figure included three duplicates, so the corrected total is 11. Josh accepted the correction and will fix the report before it goes to the client. Josh said the trend is better than last quarter and thanked the crews for filing reports promptly.

3. Near miss on the 6 March night shift
A loose drill rod fell about two metres from the mast at Pad 4. Nobody was hurt. Tariq will finish the investigation by Friday. The group agreed to use photos of the pad and the rod in a toolbox talk. One of the photos shows Kyle Barnes, the offsider on shift, who has not been asked whether he is comfortable being shown. Nobody has spoken to him yet. Ben said he would be happy to ask Kyle, but only if the supervisor on the night shift agrees first. No photos are to be shown until this is settled.

4. Fatigue roster
Tariq proposed moving the night crew from 12 hour to 10 hour shifts from 1 April. Lina opposed on the ground that it would cut crew availability. It was moved by Tariq and seconded by Maree. Carried three votes to two. Josh later raised that the client's contract specifies 12 hour shifts and the change may breach it. The group agreed to withdraw the motion until Josh has checked the contract. The roster stays as it is for now.

5. Client audit
Ben said he heard from a friend at the client that an audit could happen in April, possibly the week of the 17th. This is not confirmed. If the audit goes ahead, all inductions must be current, and the client could stop work if any are lapsed. Lina will report on the induction register by Thursday. About 70 per cent are current at the moment, though Lina said this is a rough figure.

6. Dependence on one client
About 80 per cent of the company's work comes from one mine operator. Josh said a failed audit would be a serious problem. Maree asked what happens if they pull the contract altogether. Josh did not give an answer.

7. Gas detector calibration
Only one person, Dwayne Pitt in the workshop, is trained to calibrate the gas detectors. If he is away, the detectors cannot be certified. Someone should arrange to train a second person. No one volunteered at the meeting and no date was set.

8. Heat stress
All supervisors are to check water and shade at each pad at the start of every shift while the temperature stays above 38 degrees. Maree and Tariq will jointly review the emergency response plan for heat stress by 31 March.

9. Staffing
Ben advised that he will leave at the end of May to take a position in Perth. He will work with Lina on handover. Josh asked whether anyone in the team could step into the site coordinator role. Tariq said he might, but he is worried about his workload.

10. Other business
Maree said the new induction video is excellent and the new starters found it clear.

Next meeting Monday 27 March, 7.30 am.

Minutes prepared by Lina Voss.
