ALDRIDGE AND DAUGHTERS FUNERALS, BALLARAT
Minutes of the monthly staff meeting
Wednesday 8 March 2028, the arrangement room, 8.30 am

Present: Helen Aldridge (principal and chair), Marcus Dellavedova (funeral director), Priya Nambiar (administration), Roslyn Quigley (funeral arranger), Tony Whitcombe (fleet and mortuary assistant), Jacinta Moloney (funeral director)
Apologies: none
Minutes: Priya Nambiar

1. Welcome and recording of names
Helen said the minutes would be circulated to the whole team and a short version would go to the board of the cemetery trust, and asked whether anyone objected to their name appearing. Everyone agreed except Roslyn, who asked to be recorded as R. Quigley in anything sent outside the business. Helen accepted this. Helen also asked about photos of staff for the new website. Roslyn declined to be photographed. The others said yes, and Helen said the photographer will come in April.

2. Second hearse
Marcus moved that we replace the older hearse this year. Tony said the workshop quote for repairs was $9,500 and then corrected it to $5,900 after reading the invoice again. A new vehicle would cost about $140,000 according to the dealer, but nobody had the written quote. After discussion the vote was four in favour of repairing and replacing next year, and two in favour of replacing now. Carried. The old hearse will be repaired. Tony will book the work for the week of 20 March.

3. Chapel booking system
Priya reported that the double booking last month was caused by two people using the paper diary and the spreadsheet at the same time. It was agreed that from Monday the spreadsheet is the only record. Everyone must stop using the diary. Jacinta will check with families who have a booking over the next fortnight to be sure nothing else has been missed.

4. Flower supplier
One florist in Sebastopol supplies nearly all of our arrangements. Roslyn said the owner is retiring in June and has not yet found a buyer. Helen said that this is a real risk, since several families insist on that florist. Marcus said, hypothetically, if the shop closes we could use the wholesale market in Melbourne, but the delivery times are tight. It was agreed to look at a second florist. Roslyn will call two other florists by Friday 17 March.

5. Weekend on call
The on call roster for April is not complete. Weekends of 8 and 22 April still have no second director. Helen asked whether any of the directors could cover. Jacinta said she would take the 22nd only if she can finish early on the Friday. Nobody has offered for the 8th.

6. Staff wellbeing
Helen raised that the team has had a heavy month, with 31 funerals compared with about 24 in a normal month. She asked that everybody look out for each other, and that anyone who needs a day should speak to her. Jacinta asked whether the counselling service the trust pays for could be offered again. Helen will ask the trust, and that is her job with no date yet.

7. Training
A grief support course is on in Melbourne on 5 May. Priya and Roslyn are interested. Helen said if the budget allows she would send both, and that will be known after the end of the quarter.

8. Compliments
Helen read out a letter from the family of a gentleman who died in February, thanking Marcus and Jacinta for their care and patience. The meeting asked that it be passed on to them.

9. Other business
Tony said the car wash behind the depot was out of service again, so the vehicles are going to the one in town. Priya will email the council by next Wednesday. Tea was provided by Jacinta.

Meeting closed at 9.40 am. Next meeting Wednesday 12 April.

Actions
Tony Whitcombe: book hearse repair for the week of 20 March.
Jacinta Moloney: check upcoming chapel bookings with families.
Roslyn Quigley: call two florists by Friday 17 March.
Helen Aldridge: ask the trust about counselling, no date.
Priya Nambiar: email the council about the car wash by Wednesday 15 March.
Priya Nambiar: circulate these minutes, with R. Quigley used in the external version.
All directors: April weekend on call, 8 April still open.

Signed as a true record, Priya Nambiar, 8 March 2028
