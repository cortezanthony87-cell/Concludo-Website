ACHTERBERG CHAUDHARY FINANCIAL PLANNING
Minutes of the quarterly practice review
Tuesday 21 March 2028, boardroom, 9.00 am

Present: Rosalind Achterberg (chair, principal adviser), Dev Chaudhary (adviser), Marnie Costa (adviser assistant), Owen Lim (client services), Fiona Baxter (paraplanner)
Apologies: none
Minutes taken by Owen Lim

1. Opening
Rosalind welcomed everyone and thanked the team for a strong quarter. Fiona was thanked specifically for the way she handled the end of financial year rush for the Okafor family, and Dev noted that the client sent a thank you card.

2. Annual review fee
Rosalind proposed lifting the ongoing advice fee for new clients from $3,300 to $3,600 a year from 1 July. Dev thought it was too high given that two competitors in the area advertise around $3,000, though he said this was only what he had been told. Marnie suggested $3,500 instead. A vote was taken: Rosalind and Fiona voted for $3,600, Dev and Marnie voted for $3,500, and Owen abstained. With the chair's casting vote the fee was set at $3,600 for new clients only, with existing clients unchanged until the review in December.

3. Client numbers
Rosalind reported that the practice has 212 active households. Owen later checked the client database and corrected this to 196, with the difference being households that have not had a review for over two years. The corrected figure of 196 was accepted. Rosalind said the other 16 need to be contacted or formally closed.

4. Reviews that are overdue
It was agreed that someone should contact the 16 inactive households before the end of April. Owen and Marnie will share this task and split the list in half. Owen will take the households with surnames A to L and Marnie the rest.

5. Largest client
One client family accounts for roughly a quarter of ongoing revenue, though Rosalind said she could not give an exact percentage without checking. Dev pointed out that if they moved their business, the practice would struggle to cover costs. Rosalind said she would arrange a meeting with the family before the end of May. No date was set.

6. Paraplanning workload
Fiona raised that she is the only person who can prepare statements of advice in the new software. If she is sick or on leave at a busy time, the practice has no backup. Rosalind asked whether Marnie could be trained. Marnie said she is willing but worried she may not be ready before 30 June, the deadline for the end of financial year advice. It was agreed Fiona will start training Marnie from the first week of April, and Rosalind will review how she is going in May.

7. New client portal
Dev proposed moving all clients to the new portal on 1 May. After discussion, the proposal was withdrawn, since the software vendor has not yet confirmed the data migration and Fiona has not tested it. The item will return to the June review. Dev said that if the vendor confirms in writing by mid April, the portal could go ahead in time for 1 May, but only on that condition.

8. Compliance review
The licensee's annual file review is due by 30 June. Rosalind said it would help to have Henry Voss from the licensee's compliance team come in for a half day beforehand and look at a sample of files. Nobody has asked Henry yet whether he is available, and the team would like to check with him before settling any date. Dev to make the approach.

9. Hypothetical, hiring
Rosalind asked what would happen if the practice reached 230 households by the end of the year. Dev said it would probably justify a second paraplanner, but only if the fee increase holds. No decision was made.

10. Any other business
Marnie asked if anyone knows what happens to the insurance renewal. The renewal is due on 31 March and the quote has not come in. Owen will chase the broker today. Rosalind asked, rhetorically, how we always end up doing this at the last minute.

Actions
Owen and Marnie: contact or close the 16 inactive households, by 30 April.
Dev: approach Henry Voss, this week.
Fiona: train Marnie on statements of advice, starting the first week of April.
Owen: chase the insurance broker, today.
Rosalind: arrange the meeting with the largest client family, no date set.
Everyone: send any portal concerns to Fiona before the June review.

Next review: Tuesday 20 June 2028.
