Lakeside Terrace Owners Corporation
Minutes of committee meeting held Monday 9 August 2027 at 6:30 pm, common room

1. Attendance and quorum
Present: Ewan McAllister (chair), Fatima Haddad (secretary), Peter Nowak (treasurer), Alinta Cooper.
Apologies: Ron Dimitrov.
Also present: Lena Brandt, as proxy holder for Ron Dimitrov for item 4 only.
The secretary noted that a quorum requires three committee members. Four were present, so quorum was met. Lena Brandt's proxy does not count toward the quorum.
These minutes will be circulated to all lot owners. Lena asked that her name not appear on the circulated version for item 4, and the committee agreed. Alinta was not asked whether she was comfortable being named in item 6.

2. Previous minutes
Moved Fatima Haddad, seconded Peter Nowak, that the minutes of 12 July be accepted. Carried.

3. Treasurer's report
Peter reported the administrative fund balance as roughly $14,000 and the sinking fund as about $61,500, though the bank statement had not arrived. Two lots are behind on levies, one by $1,850 and one by about $600. Peter will send reminder notices by 20 August.
Alinta said the committee should consider whether the late fees are high enough, but there was no motion.

4. Roof repairs
Quote received from Eastgate Roofing for $23,400 to repair the flashing on the north block. A second quote has been requested but has not arrived.
Motion 1: Moved Ewan McAllister, seconded Alinta Cooper, that the committee accept the Eastgate quote of $23,400.
Amendment, moved Peter Nowak, seconded Fatima Haddad: that acceptance be conditional on the second quote arriving by 31 August and being no lower by more than 10 percent.
The amendment was put and carried 3 to 1.
The amended motion was then put and carried 3 to 1.
Ewan will tell Eastgate by Friday that the committee intends to accept subject to the condition. Fatima will chase the second contractor for a quote.
Peter noted that all the repair funds are dependent on the sinking fund, which has no other buffer.

5. Visitor parking
Motion 2: Moved Alinta Cooper, seconded Peter Nowak, that visitor parking be limited to four hours between 8 am and 6 pm. The motion was put and lost, 1 for, 3 against. The committee did not want to introduce limits without a survey.
Ewan asked whether owners would accept a trial. Not answered.

6. Garden maintenance
The contract with the current gardener expires on 30 September. Ewan suggested a possible switch to a monthly rather than fortnightly visit if the price drops. No decision. Fatima and Alinta to each get a quote from a different gardener, with no date set.
Everyone present praised the recent work on the front entry planting.

7. Insurance renewal
The building insurance renews on 1 October. Peter said this date cannot move. The broker has asked for the updated valuation, which only Peter has the contact for. If Peter is unavailable, no one else knows who to call. Fatima said she would ask Peter to write down the details, but no one was given a date.

8. Matters referred
Ron Dimitrov submitted a written proposal to change the by-laws on pets. The committee agreed this affects all owners and should be considered at the next annual general meeting. The matter is referred to the AGM and no decision was made.

9. Correspondence
A letter from the lot 14 owner complaining about noise from the pool pump. Ewan to reply, without delay if possible. Fatima asked whether the pump was ever serviced. Nobody knew.
A short chat about whether the new bin enclosure looked too grey. It was dismissed.

10. Other business
None.

The meeting closed at 8:05 pm. Next meeting: Monday 13 September 2027 at 6:30 pm.

Action list
Peter Nowak: reminder notices to late-paying lots by 20 August.
Ewan McAllister: contact Eastgate Roofing by Friday.
Fatima Haddad: chase second roofing quote.
Fatima Haddad and Alinta Cooper: each get one gardener quote, no date.
Ewan McAllister: reply to lot 14 owner.
