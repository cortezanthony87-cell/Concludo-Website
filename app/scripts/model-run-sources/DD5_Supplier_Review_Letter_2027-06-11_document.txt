Dear Mr Subramaniam,

Review meeting of 9 June 2027

Thank you for coming to our workshop on Wednesday. I write to record what Keiran Doyle and I understood to be agreed, and what is still open. Present for Eastland were you and Ms Lakshmi Parekh. For Harbourside, Keiran Doyle (production manager) and I attended. I thank you both for your frankness about the supply difficulties earlier this year.

Prices. You offered to hold the current price on kiln-dried hardwood until the end of September, and we accepted. We did not accept your proposal to move to a fixed monthly order of 40 cubic metres, because our volumes are too uneven. Eastland has therefore agreed to keep ordering on a job-by-job basis. At the meeting I had suggested that we might later consider a rebate for larger volumes, but that was only a thought, and I do not regard it as an offer.

Deliveries. Late deliveries have been, in my experience, the worst feature of the last year. You said that, in your view, delivery within three working days is the standard Eastland meets. Our records show that in March and April it was nearer nine. Please correct me if the figures differ on your side. We agreed that Ms Parekh will send me the delivery log for March to May.

Quality. Keiran raised the cracked boards in the last shipment. You said that a warped board is never the supplier's fault once it has left the yard, and Keiran disagreed. We have not settled the point. It remains a difference between us.

Credit. Your account department told us last week that our balance is overdue by 21 days. We believe the amount is mostly an invoice that was issued twice, and we will pay the undisputed amount at once.

Named contacts. Eastland asked whether it could use our company name as a reference in its marketing. We declined, for now. Mr Subramaniam also asked us to name our Eastland contact when speaking to the industry association. I told you we would rather not be named.

A note on dependence. Nearly half of our hardwood comes from Eastland. If your yard closed or your mill were damaged we would have no alternative source, and I should say that plainly.

Schedule of actions
1. Harbourside to pay the undisputed part of the account by Friday 18 June (Harbourside, Keiran Doyle).
2. Eastland to send the delivery log for March to May by Friday 25 June (Ms Parekh).
3. Eastland to credit the duplicated invoice once confirmed (Mr Subramaniam; no date set).
4. Harbourside and Eastland to inspect the cracked boards together at the Dandenong yard (Keiran Doyle and Ms Parekh, both to arrange a day).
5. Harbourside to send a forecast of orders for July to September by 2 July (Jacinta Kowalczyk).
6. Eastland is not to ship any hardwood to our Geelong yard before the account has been cleared (Mr Subramaniam).
7. Review the price hold again in the first week of September (all four).

We were pleased with the way you handled the meeting, and Keiran in particular valued your offer to visit the sawmill.
