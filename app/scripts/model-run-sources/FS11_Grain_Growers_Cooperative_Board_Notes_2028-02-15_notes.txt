Wimmera Plains Grain Growers Cooperative, committee meeting notes
Tuesday 15 February 2028, 7.30 pm, the shed at the Kaniva receival site

Present: Graham Tolhurst (chair), Pauline Eastwood (secretary), Rohan Mistry (treasurer), Deb Kowalski (silo manager), Wayne Hartigan (member representative)
Apologies: none. Marcia Lennox (agronomist) was not at the meeting.
Notes taken by Pauline.

Photos and names. Pauline said she wants to put the harvest photos and the names of the committee in the March newsletter. Graham, Rohan, Deb and Wayne said fine. Pauline said that Marcia is in two of the photos and nobody has asked her yet. Pauline will ask her before anything goes out.

1. Harvest receival tonnage
Deb reported 61,000 tonnes received this season. Rohan checked the weighbridge summary during the meeting and the final figure is 57,400 tonnes, so the 61,000 was wrong and has been corrected in these notes. Wayne said the neighbouring co-op is saying their total is up 10 per cent, but that is just talk at the pub.

2. Storage fee
Rohan proposed lifting the storage fee from $4.50 to $6 a tonne. Wayne thought $6 was too steep. After a long argument they settled on $5.25 a tonne from 1 July. Vote was three for and two against, so it is carried. Graham asked Rohan to write up a short letter to members explaining why.

3. Bunker repair
If the insurer pays out, the cracked bunker wall at the northern site gets fixed in April. Deb said she would not start the job without the payout in writing. Graham said, and this is the main worry, that all of our grain going out in March goes through that one bunker and one loader, so if either one fails we are stuck. Nobody had a plan for that yet. Deb will ask the contractor whether a second loader can be hired for a fortnight.

4. Buyer concentration
About 70 per cent of the wheat goes to one buyer in Portland. Graham said he would like another buyer. Wayne asked what happens if that buyer drops our price by even a few dollars. Nobody answered. Graham will arrange a meeting with a second buyer, no date yet.

5. Field day
Should we hold the field day in the first or second week of April? Pauline moved first week, Wayne wanted the second. Deferred until Deb checks when the sheep sale is. Deb will let everyone know by Friday.

6. New moisture meter
Quote is $3,200. Rohan said he would buy it if the cash flow allows. Deb noted that if it is not here by March the old one has to be recalibrated, which costs money and takes two days. Rohan and Deb to sort the ordering between them.

7. Other business
Wayne said it was a good season and everyone did well. Pauline thanked Deb for keeping the weighbridge queue moving during the rush. A short chat about the footy tipping, then we closed at 9.15 pm.

Actions
Pauline: ask Marcia about her photo before the newsletter, by the end of February.
Rohan: letter to members about the storage fee, by Friday 25 February.
Deb: ask the contractor about a second loader.
Deb: confirm the sheep sale date, by Friday.
Rohan and Deb: moisture meter ordering.
Graham: second buyer meeting, no date.
Someone needs to chase the insurer for the payout letter. Nobody took it.
Next meeting: Tuesday 15 March.
