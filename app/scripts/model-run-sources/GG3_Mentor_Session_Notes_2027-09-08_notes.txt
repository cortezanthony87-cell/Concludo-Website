Mentor session notes: Wednesday 8 September 2027
Founder: Isabel Quinlan (Kestrel Bakehouse wholesale arm)
My notes, written straight after the session.

8 Sep, opening.
Isabel arrived late and a bit frazzled. We chatted about her daughter's netball finals for ten minutes before getting to business. She was in good spirits, though.

8 Sep, hiring.
Isabel told me she had decided to hire a second delivery driver. I asked what it would cost and she said about fifty-five thousand dollars a year, maybe sixty with on-costs, and I think the real figure is higher. I suggested she wait until the new cafe contract was signed. By the end of the session she reversed her decision and said she would not hire until the contract is in writing. I agreed that was the safer course.

8 Sep, the big customer.
Roughly sixty per cent of her revenue comes from one chain of cafes. If they walk, she is in serious trouble. I said every bakery in this position has a day where the biggest customer rings and says they are going elsewhere. She said it could never happen to her because they have been loyal for years. I do not think "never" is a safe word here. I suggested she land two more mid-sized customers this year. Isabel will draw up a list of ten target cafes by next Wednesday.

8 Sep, cash.
A supplier invoice for flour, about eleven thousand dollars, is thirty days overdue. Isabel said she is just waiting for a payment from the chain to come in. I told her to ring the flour supplier today and ask for an extension, and not to wait. Isabel agreed to ring them tomorrow morning.

8 Sep, what if.
We talked through a what-if. If the chain cut its order by a third, would the business survive the quarter? Isabel thought so, because she has about two months of cash, though she was not certain. I asked her to run the numbers properly. Nobody has been given the job of checking the lease on the second oven, which is a loose end. I mentioned it but we did not settle who would do it.

8 Sep, advisers.
I asked whether her accountant has seen the cash forecast. She said no. Why not? She said she had not had time. I told her this is a common trap. I suggested she book an hour with the accountant before the end of the month.

8 Sep, sharing.
Isabel asked me not to pass these notes on to her investor, Mr Pellegrino, until she has decided what to tell him. I said that was fine. She was happy for me to show a short summary to my mentoring coordinator, though.

8 Sep, praise.
I told Isabel her new sourdough range photos looked excellent and the packaging is much improved. She was pleased. Her staff turnover is low, which is another positive.

8 Sep, close.
Next session in a fortnight, same time. I will send her the template for a customer concentration plan after this one. I will also introduce her to a cafe group owner I know, subject to his agreeing.
