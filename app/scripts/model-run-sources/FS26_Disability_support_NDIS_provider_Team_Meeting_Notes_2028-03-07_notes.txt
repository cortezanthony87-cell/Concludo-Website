Harbourlight Support Services, team leaders' meeting
Tuesday 7 March 2028, 9.30 am, Dandenong office

Attendees: Rosalind Okafor (service manager), Tran Bao Ngoc (team leader, east), Gareth Pemberton (team leader, south), Ayesha Siddiqui (roster coordinator), Joel Mavrikos (compliance officer)

1. Roster pressures
Ayesha said we are short 40 support hours a week across both teams, mostly weekday evenings. Gareth said it was closer to 35 hours, but Ayesha stuck with 40 for now until she has finished the new roster. Rosalind asked everyone to flag any shift they cannot fill by Thursday each week. Agreed.

2. Worker leaving
Mele, a long serving support worker in the east team, has told Ngoc she is thinking of leaving after Easter. She has not resigned. Ngoc is worried the two participants she supports on Thursday evenings will take it hard, as they have a settled routine. Rosalind said nobody should speak about it with participants until Mele confirms.

3. Incident reporting
Joel reminded the group that reportable incidents must go to the NDIS Commission within the required timeframe, and that two incident forms last month were filed late. Rosalind said all team leaders must check the incident inbox every morning. No single person owns this, it sits with whoever opens the office first. Gareth asked if that is clear enough. Rosalind said she would think about it.

4. Participant newsletter and photos
Ngoc proposed including photos from the Frankston beach day in the next newsletter. Joel said photos need written consent from each participant or their guardian before use. Ngoc said most families have signed, but she believes the guardian of one participant, Wendell Achterberg, has not yet been asked. Wendell's guardian is his sister, Corinne. Nobody has contacted her. Decision: photos of Wendell stay out until Corinne has been asked. Ngoc to ask Corinne this week.

5. Notetaking in this meeting
Rosalind asked whether everyone is happy for Ayesha to take notes and use first names. Gareth said yes, and the rest nodded. Joel asked that his name not be recorded against the compliance comments. Rosalind agreed and the compliance points are noted as from the compliance officer only.

6. Vehicle
The south team van has failed its roadworthy check on the brakes. Gareth said the quote for repairs is $1,850, though the mechanic might drop it a little. If it is done by Friday, the van can be used for the outing on Saturday. Otherwise the outing is cancelled or the team uses private cars. Rosalind said private cars are not an option because of insurance. Gareth to book the repair today.

7. Training
Mandatory manual handling refresher: Joel proposed two sessions, 21 March and 28 March. Vote taken, three for one session on 21 March only, two against. One session on 21 March goes ahead. Gareth said it will be tight for casuals.

8. Positive feedback
A participant's mother called to say the new support worker in the south team is wonderful. Rosalind asked that this be passed on to the worker.

9. Funding review
Rosalind said the plan reviews for four participants fall in April and, depending on the outcomes, hours may drop. This is a concern but nothing is confirmed. No action yet.
