HARBOURVIEW STRATA MANAGEMENT
Minutes of the strata committee meeting, Owners Corporation Plan 4417 (Kestrel Court, 22 apartments, Footscray)
Friday 24 March 2028, 6.00 pm, building common room

Present: Helen Yiannakis (chair, Lot 3), Raymond Cho (Lot 12), Sunita Patel (Lot 18), Owen Blackwood (Lot 7), Daniel Ferreira (strata manager, Harbourview)
Apologies: none

1. Opening
The chair opened the meeting at 6.05 pm. Owen apologised for the state of the common room carpet, which he said is a reason in itself to have the meeting. Minutes of the previous meeting were taken as read and confirmed.

2. Insurance renewal
Daniel reported that the building insurance falls due on 30 April and the insurer will not extend cover past that date. He said the renewal quote was $31,800. After checking his papers during the meeting he corrected this to $28,900, the figure on the second page of the quote. The committee noted the corrected figure. Raymond asked whether a different broker could do better. Daniel said it is possible but he could not promise it, and there is only five weeks to the deadline. It was agreed to accept the renewal unless a cheaper written quote arrives by Friday 7 April. Raymond will ask one other broker. Daniel will chase the insurer for a copy of the certificate of currency.

3. Lift
The lift has stopped between floors twice in the past month. All lift servicing is done by one contractor, Northgate Lifts, and Daniel said the company has only two technicians who cover this area. If one is on leave, the building could wait days for a callout. Sunita said she had heard that Northgate is being bought by a bigger firm, but she could not say where she heard it, so it was recorded as hearsay. Helen asked whether anyone knows what happens to the contract if that happens. Nobody did, and Daniel will find out by the next meeting. Owen suggested that, hypothetically, if the lift is out for a long time, the committee would need a plan for owners who cannot use stairs. Helen asked all owners on the upper floors to let the committee know quietly if they need help, and Daniel will include a line in the next newsletter.

4. Roof leak above Lot 20
A leak was reported above Lot 20 after heavy rain. The roofer quoted $4,500 to repair the flashing. Owen moved that the work go ahead immediately. Sunita seconded. Raymond voted against, saying the committee should get a second quote. Carried three to one, with the chair abstaining. Daniel to book the roofer for the first dry week.

5. Special levy
Raymond proposed a special levy of $1,200 per lot to build a reserve for the façade repairs. Sunita supported it. Helen said she thought it was too soon after the last levy and several owners would struggle. After discussion Raymond withdrew the proposal. It was agreed that Daniel will prepare a ten year plan for the sinking fund and put it in front of the next meeting. The levy will be reconsidered then, if the plan shows a shortfall.

6. Rubbish room and bin contract
The council has asked all buildings to separate food waste from March next year. Nobody has been asked to look into what this will mean for the bin room, and no one volunteered. The item stays open.

7. Garden
Sunita and Owen will jointly arrange for the courtyard garden to be tidied before Easter. They will share the cost of a landscaper up to $600. Helen said the courtyard looks wonderful already and thanked Sunita for the new plants.

8. Correspondence
A letter from the owner of Lot 14 complained about noise from the Lot 15 renovation. Daniel will write to both owners this week. The chair noted that this is the third complaint about the same lot in six months.

9. Other business
Raymond noted that the intercom is still not working properly. Daniel will report back by email when the technician has been. No date was given.

Meeting closed at 7.40 pm. Next meeting is to be held on Friday 12 May 2028 at 6.00 pm in the common room.

Minutes prepared by:
Daniel Ferreira
Strata Manager, Harbourview Strata Management
