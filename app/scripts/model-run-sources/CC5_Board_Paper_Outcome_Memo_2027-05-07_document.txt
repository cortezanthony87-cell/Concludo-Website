MEMORANDUM

To: All staff and volunteers, Gippsland Meals Together Incorporated
From: The Secretary, on behalf of the Board
Date: 7 May 2027
Subject: Outcomes of the Board meeting held on Tuesday 4 May 2027

This memorandum summarises the outcomes of the Board meeting held on 4 May 2027. It is provided for information. It is not the formal minute, which will be circulated once the Board confirms it at its next meeting.

1. Delivery van
At its March meeting the Board resolved to sell the older delivery van and lease a replacement. At the May meeting the Board reversed that resolution. The Board noted that the lease quotes had risen and that the older van has passed its recent inspection. The van will be retained for at least twelve months. The Treasurer is to obtain a written estimate for the cost of the tyres by 28 May.

2. Meal pricing
The Board considered a proposal to increase the price of a standard meal from nine dollars to eleven dollars. The proposal was put to a vote and was carried by four votes to two. The new price takes effect on 1 July 2027. Two Board members recorded that they were opposed, on the grounds that some clients may stop ordering. The Board accepts that this is a possible risk and has not estimated the effect.

3. Volunteer coordinator position
A motion to create a paid volunteer coordinator position was withdrawn by its mover before a vote. The mover indicated that the motion would be brought back when the budget for the next year is known. No date has been fixed.

4. Kitchen refurbishment
The Board resolved to defer the kitchen refurbishment decision until the grant outcome is known. The grant applicant has said the outcome should arrive in about six weeks, although this is not confirmed.

5. Client dependence
The Board noted that the local council contract accounts for roughly half of the organisation's income. The Board considers this a significant dependence on a single funder. The Chair is to meet the council officer in June to discuss renewal terms.

6. Overdue amounts
The Treasurer reported that two invoices to partner organisations are now more than ninety days overdue. The Treasurer is to send a final reminder by 14 May. The Board did not resolve to take further action.

7. Rejected proposal
A proposal to relocate the head office to rented premises in Sale was put to a vote and was rejected by five votes to one. The Board stated that the existing premises remain suitable.

8. Staff matters
The Board recorded its thanks to the kitchen team for the positive client survey results. The Board also noted that the longest serving cook has indicated an intention to retire at the end of the year, which is a risk to continuity. The Secretary is to prepare a succession note, no date set.

9. Recording and publication
The meeting was audio recorded with the consent of all present, except that one Board member declined to be recorded and asked that their remarks be taken from the written notes only. The Board did not discuss whether the memorandum may be shared with the funders.

10. Next meeting
The next meeting will be held on Tuesday 1 June 2027.

Staff are asked not to discuss the pricing change with clients until the Secretary has sent the approved client letter.
