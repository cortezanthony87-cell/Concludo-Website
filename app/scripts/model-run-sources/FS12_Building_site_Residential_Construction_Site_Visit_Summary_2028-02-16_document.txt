Thanks for coming down to site this morning, and thanks to Sunil and Tom for making the time. Here is where we landed, written up while it is fresh. Please shout if I have got anything wrong.

1. Slab and frame
The slab passed inspection on Monday and Tom's crew will start the frame on Monday 28 February. Tom said the crew did a great job keeping the site tidy through the wet week, and Ian agreed it looks far better than most sites he has walked past.

2. Window colour
Ian wanted the black frames and Paula preferred the monument grey. After a bit of back and forth we settled on black for the front and grey for the rear of the house, as a compromise. Paula then wondered if that would look odd from the side. Sunil is going to mock it up and send a drawing so you can both decide, so please treat the colour as not final until you have seen it.

3. Cost of the upgraded windows
I said on the day that the upgrade adds $6,400. Sorry, that is wrong. The correct figure is $4,600, which is what is in the supplier quote. I will send you the quote tomorrow.

4. Roof trusses
All the trusses come from one fabricator in Dandenong, and they have only one delivery slot a week. If their factory has a delay we have nothing to fall back on. Tom thinks we would lose about a week, maybe ten days, but he has not asked them. Dean to ring the fabricator and ask what their backup is by Friday.

5. Plumbing rough in
Kerry Ann can rough in the bathroom as soon as the frame is up. If the frame goes ahead on the 28th, she will be on site in the second week of March. Her promise holds only if the frame inspection is booked by the first of March, so Tom, please make sure that happens.

6. Handover date
Paula and Ian, I know you need the house by the end of August because your lease ends on 31 August. That date is firm on your side. On ours, the plan works only if the weather behaves and the trusses arrive on time. I am not going to promise August until I have heard back from the fabricator.

7. Driveway
Ian asked whether the neighbours' fence needs to come down for the concrete pump. We agreed that someone should talk to the neighbours at number 14. Nobody put their hand up at the meeting, so I am raising it again here. Could one of you let me know if you are happy for me to knock on their door, or whether you would prefer to do it yourselves?

8. Hypothetical, the extra bedroom
If you later decide to add the study as a fourth bedroom, we would need to change the electrical layout before the frame is closed in. That is a decision for later and costs nothing to leave open for now, but it has a deadline of about the end of March.

9. Site safety
Everyone on site, including visitors, must sign in and wear boots and hi vis. Please remind your family that the site is not a place for the kids to visit on weekends.

10. Photos
I took a few photos of the slab and the frame setup today. With your permission I would like to share them with Sunil and the engineer, and possibly use a couple on our website once the job is done, without your address. Is that all right with you? I will hold off until I hear from you.
