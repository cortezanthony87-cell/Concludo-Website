Dear Bronwyn and Lachlan,

Thank you both for having me out to your place yesterday, and for the tea. It was good to meet Biscuit as well, although I think he is still sulking about the ladder. 1. The system size. At the visit I said that your north facing roof could take a 6.6 kW array. After going back through my measurements, that was wrong. The usable space is smaller than I thought because of the chimney and the vent stack, so the right number is 5.5 kW, which is 14 panels. Sorry about the mistake. The quote I send you will use 5.5 kW.

2. The price. For a 5.5 kW system with a battery I expect the total to come in at about $13,900, though I would not hold me to that until the quote is final. Without the battery it would be around $7,200. Those figures include the switchboard upgrade I mentioned, but only if your existing board is the older ceramic type. If it turns out to be a newer one, the price drops by around $600.

3. The rebate. The federal rebate applies at the time of installation, and I explained that the amount reduces each year. If we install before the end of December, I expect the discount to be about $2,300. After that it falls. I can't promise the number, because it depends on the figures the government publishes, so please treat it as an estimate. If you go ahead, you will need to sign the paperwork by Wednesday 1 December for me to be sure of a December date.

4. The inverter. The inverter we would use comes from one manufacturer only, and they are currently running about six weeks behind on deliveries. If that does not improve, your installation could slip into February. I'll know more on Monday, and I will ring you the same day either way. If it does slip, we could look at a different brand, but I haven't priced that.

5. Roof photos. I took about thirty photos of the roof and the switchboard. I would like to send these to our designer, Suresh, so he can draw up the layout. If either of you would rather I didn't share them with him, tell me before Monday and I will not. I will not use them in our marketing, and I will not put your names or your address on anything we publish.

6. What I need from you. Could you please send me a copy of your last electricity bill by Tuesday? Lachlan, you mentioned you might be able to find the old switchboard diagram in the garage. If you do find it, it would help a lot, but don't go to any trouble.

7. What I will do. I will send the quote by Thursday 2 December. Suresh will have the layout drawn by the end of next week. I'll also book the electrician, but I can only do that once you have signed.

8. A question you asked. You asked whether the system would still work in a blackout. Without the battery, no. With the battery, yes, but only to run the essentials, and only for a few hours. It will not run the whole house, and I would not want you to expect that. Is that what you had in mind? If not, let me know, and we can talk about a bigger battery.

9. A general comment. Does anyone really enjoy reading a solar quote? I have tried to keep ours as clear as I can, so please call me if any part of it isn't.

Thanks again for your time. It is a lovely home, and I think the system will suit it well.
