HARBOUR AND ROSS SURVEYORS
Minutes of the partners' meeting held on Monday 8 March 2027 at 4.00 pm, Newcastle office

Present: Margaret Ross (Chair), Anil Sharma, Joel Whitaker, Yasmin Park
In attendance: Priscilla Aked (office manager, minutes)
Apologies: nil

1. Declaration and recording
1.1 The Chair noted that the minutes would be taken by Ms Aked from handwritten notes only. No audio recording was made. All partners agreed to the minutes being circulated to the firm's accountant.

2. Confirmation of previous minutes
2.1 The minutes of 8 February 2027 were confirmed without amendment.

3. Business arising
3.1 Mr Sharma reported that the drone licence renewal had been lodged on 24 February. Noted.

4. Hunter Valley subdivision project
4.1 Mr Whitaker reported that the project for Valley Rise Developments represents about half of the firm's current billings. He cautioned that the estimate may be too high because two invoices are not yet issued.
4.2 Ms Ross observed that dependence on one developer is a risk. Mr Sharma, Mr Whitaker and Ms Park agreed.
4.3 Resolved that the firm will seek at least two new clients of a similar size by 30 September 2027. Carried unanimously.
4.4 Mr Whitaker is to prepare a list of five target developers for the next meeting.

5. Overdue accounts
5.1 Ms Aked reported that $18,600 is outstanding from the Cessnock council contract, the oldest invoice being 112 days overdue.
5.2 Mr Sharma asked whether the firm should charge interest. Ms Ross said that has never been the firm's practice and asked whether anyone really wanted to start now. No answer was given. The question was treated as rhetorical.
5.3 Ms Aked is to send a final demand to the council by Friday 12 March. Mr Sharma is to ring the council's finance officer in the same week. Mr Whitaker is not to contact the council directly on this matter.

6. Purchase of a new total station
6.1 Mr Sharma moved that the firm purchase a new total station at a cost of approximately $22,000. Mr Whitaker seconded.
6.2 Ms Ross opposed on the ground that the existing instrument is serviceable until at least 2028.
6.3 The motion was put. For: Mr Sharma and Mr Whitaker. Against: Ms Ross. Ms Park abstained. Carried two votes to one.
6.4 Mr Sharma is to obtain two written quotes. No date was set.

7. Appointment of a graduate surveyor
7.1 Mr Whitaker moved that the firm advertise for a graduate surveyor starting July 2027. Ms Ross, Mr Sharma and Ms Park opposed. The motion was lost.
7.2 Mr Sharma noted that Ms Ross intends to reduce her days from July and that the firm will then rely heavily on two licensed partners. Noted.

8. Office lease and parking
8.1 The Chair stated that item 8 would be held over to the next meeting as the landlord's letter has not yet arrived.

9. Software licence
9.1 The partners tied two to two on whether to upgrade the CAD software (Mr Sharma and Ms Ross for; Mr Whitaker and Ms Park against). The Chair used her casting vote for the upgrade. Carried on the casting vote of the Chair.
9.2 Ms Aked is to book the upgrade for the first week of April.

10. Other business
10.1 Ms Ross thanked Ms Aked for the clear handling of the end of financial year files, which saved the firm a considerable amount of time.
10.2 Ms Aked said she was worried about the workload in June. Mr Sharma said he would look at temporary help if it became necessary. No decision was made.

11. Close
11.1 The meeting closed at 5.20 pm. The next meeting will be held on Monday 12 April 2027.
