FILE NOTE

Matter: Home loan application, refinance
Prepared by: Gabriella Marino, Mortgage Broker, Marino Home Finance
Date of meeting: 9 July 2027, 10:00 am
Attendees: Gabriella Marino; Mr Daniel Okoye; Ms Chloe Okoye

1. Purpose of meeting
The clients attended to discuss refinancing their existing home loan of approximately $540,000. They are seeking a lower interest rate and have indicated that they would like the process to be completed before the end of September.

2. Discussion
Mr Okoye stated that their current lender has never been willing to negotiate on rate. This is the clients' recollection, and no written record was seen.

Ms Okoye advised that her income may fall from August when she reduces her hours. The figure of $78,000 was quoted but is to be confirmed from her payslips.

The clients asked whether they could also borrow extra for a renovation of roughly $60,000. The broker explained that this is a possibility only, and that assessment depends on the valuation. No commitment was given.

The clients raised a concern about the fixed rate period of their present loan, which ends on 30 September 2027. The broker noted that break costs may apply if they refinance before that date. This date is fixed by the existing lender.

3. Proposal considered
The broker proposed an offset account feature with the new loan. Ms Okoye was in favour, Mr Okoye was not and preferred a lower rate with no offset. The clients agreed a compromise: the broker will price both options and the clients will choose after seeing the comparison.

The broker also suggested that the clients consider consolidating their car loan. The clients formally rejected this proposal.

4. Privacy
The broker confirmed the clients' consent to obtain a credit report. The clients were told that their documents would be stored securely. Consent to share information with a valuer was not discussed at this meeting.

5. Concerns
The broker noted that the clients' account with the existing lender shows two late repayments in the last twelve months. The broker has no information on the reasons. Further, the clients depend on a single income if Ms Okoye's hours fall, which may affect borrowing capacity.

The clients were asked not to contact the valuer directly. All contact should go through the broker's office.

8. Other matters
Mr Okoye asked whether it is a smart idea to take a cash back offer. The broker said that was a matter for another day. Ms Okoye remarked that the clients are very happy with the service provided to date.

This note records the broker's own understanding of the meeting and is not a statement agreed by the clients.
