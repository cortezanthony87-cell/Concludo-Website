Harbour Street Physio, team meeting
Thursday 5 August 2027, 12:30 to 1:15
Typed by Noelene Ferris, practice manager
Present: Noelene, Dr Hamid Saberi, Chloe Ng, Warren Pask (reception), Imogen Tran (bookkeeper, joined by phone)

1. Opening
Hamid thanked everyone for covering during the school holidays. Chloe got a lovely card from a patient's family after her last session, read out. Everyone agreed the new waiting room chairs are a hit.

2. Thursday late opening
Proposal from Hamid: open until 7 pm on Thursdays instead of 5 pm. Chloe was against it because of her childcare. Warren and Imogen were both in favour, and Noelene was undecided. Voted: three for, one against, one abstained (Noelene). Carried. Starts 2 September for a trial of eight weeks. Chloe will work the early shift on Thursdays instead.
Noelene to update the booking system by the end of this month.

3. Friday closure
Last month we decided to close on Fridays from October. Hamid said he now thinks this was a mistake because of the number of Friday bookings. Reversed: the Friday closure decision is cancelled. Fridays stay open. Noelene to let the Friday patients on the waiting list know, no date set.

4. New booking software
Imogen asked whether we should look at moving to a different system, since the current one charges a fee for each reminder text. Hamid said it might cost more to switch than we save. Deferred until after the Thursday trial. Nobody is to sign up for anything before then. Warren asked whether any of the options sync with the accounting package, no one knew.

5. Laser unit
Only Chloe is trained on the laser unit. If she is away, those patients cannot be seen. Hamid said this is a risk we have to fix. Hamid and Warren are to look at training courses together and report next meeting.
Some discussion about whether to buy a second unit. Hamid suggested it as an idea only, "if we had a very good quarter". Not proposed formally and withdrawn before a vote.

6. Patient photos
Chloe asked if we can start taking progress photos for knee rehab patients. Hamid said every patient must be asked first and sign the form. Noelene to draft a consent form. Warren has not been asked about handling the photo files, to be raised with him later.
Imogen asked whether the photos would be stored on the shared drive. Open question, not answered.

7. Money
Imogen reported that two insurers are late paying. Total outstanding is about $8,000, possibly more. One account is 60 days overdue. Noelene to chase both, Imogen to send her the list.
Warren asked why the insurers always pay late. Hamid said "who knows" and the item moved on.
Imogen noted that a fifth of our revenue comes from one employer's health scheme, which makes us dependent on their contract renewal.

8. Staff room fridge
Noelene noted the fridge needs cleaning out every Friday. Everyone laughed. No one volunteered, and it was left.
