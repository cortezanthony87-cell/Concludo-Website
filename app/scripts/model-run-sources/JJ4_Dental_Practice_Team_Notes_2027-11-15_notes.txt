Team meeting notes, Wattle Street Dental, Monday 15 November 2027

Attendees: Dr Anh Tran (principal dentist), Dr Marcus Weller, Natasha Ioannou (practice manager), Hannah Bright (dental assistant), Omar Haddad (dental assistant)

The meeting opened with small talk about the long weekend. Hannah had been at the Mornington Peninsula and said it rained the entire time. The notes below cover the business items.

Sterilisation equipment. Dr Tran explained that the main autoclave has been giving error codes on and off for about three weeks. The technician quoted around $6,200 to replace the heating element and the control board. Later in the meeting, when Natasha checked the quote, she found the figure was actually $6,800, with the extra amount being for the board. Dr Weller asked whether it would be cheaper to buy a new machine, and Dr Tran said it probably would not be, but she has not priced one. Nobody wanted to decide today. Dr Tran will ask the technician for a written quote by Thursday and the team will revisit it then. In the meantime, the practice only has one autoclave that works reliably, so if that fails, the clinic would have to stop sterilising on site. Natasha said this is the most worrying thing on the list.

Rostering trial. Natasha proposed trialling a split roster for the assistants for the first month of next year. Hannah would work Tuesday to Friday and Omar would work Monday to Thursday. Both assistants said they were happy to try it. It was agreed that Hannah will begin on the new roster from 3 January, and Omar will begin on the new roster from 3 January too. Natasha will email the final roster to both of them by the end of this week.

Dr Weller's departure. Dr Weller told the team he will finish up at the end of January. He said he has accepted a position closer to home. Dr Tran thanked him and said he had been a great colleague. She asked how patients would be told. Dr Weller said he would prefer patients hear it from him directly rather than from reception. Natasha will draft a short script for reception staff to use if patients ask before he has spoken to them. Dr Tran said she is a little concerned that one dentist leaving in a six-chair practice might leave the appointment book thin through February, though that is only her estimate.

Patient feedback. Natasha read out some feedback from the survey. A patient said the team made her feel comfortable with a long procedure. Dr Tran said it was good to hear, and Hannah said she would pass it on to the new graduate who helped in that appointment. Natasha then mentioned that a few patients have complained about long waits on Thursday afternoons, but only two or three, so it may not be a pattern.

Sharing the notes. Natasha asked if the notes from this meeting could be shared with the locum who will be starting in February. Dr Tran said yes. Omar said he was not comfortable with his comments about the roster being shared with someone outside the team. The group agreed that the version sent to the locum would leave out the rostering section.

Newsletter. Natasha asked if the practice could name Dr Tran in the spring newsletter as the dentist who recently completed the advanced implant course. Dr Tran said she would rather not be named. It was agreed that the newsletter will mention the course but no one by name.

Questions raised. Hannah asked whether the clinic will close over Christmas as it did last year. Natasha said she is still waiting to hear from the building owner and will let everyone know. Omar asked whether anyone knows if the new gloves have arrived. Nobody knew. Dr Weller asked, to no one in particular, why the supplier is always late in November.

Actions. Natasha to get the autoclave decision back on the agenda for Thursday. Hannah to restock the consumables cupboard when the order arrives. Someone needs to order the new gloves, but nobody put their hand up.

Next team meeting: Monday 29 November.
