TIDEWATER KAYAK HIRE PTY LTD
Minutes of the Board Retreat
Wednesday 17 November 2027, held at the Lorne boatshed

Present: Sandra Whitlock (Chair), Peter Oduya, Catherine Bell, Anil Deshmukh, Fiona Rakoczy (Company Secretary)

Apologies: Tomasz Brandt, Meredith Yu

1. Opening and conflicts
The Chair opened the meeting at 9:10 am. No conflicts of interest were declared. The Chair noted that these minutes may be shared with the staff after approval, and no member objected.

2. Previous minutes
The minutes of the October meeting were taken as read.

3. Season outlook
Peter Oduya reported that bookings for December were up on last year, though he said the figure of around thirty percent was an estimate from the booking system and has not been checked. Catherine Bell cautioned that one cruise operator provides most of the group bookings, and if they pulled out, the summer forecast would look very different. The Board noted the risk.

4. Fleet replacement
It was proposed by Anil Deshmukh and seconded by Catherine Bell that the company buy eight new double kayaks before Christmas at a cost of $14,400. Peter Oduya asked whether the figure included delivery. Anil Deshmukh said he believed it did, but he would check. The motion was carried, three in favour and one against, with Sandra Whitlock abstaining.

5. Reversal of the October decision on the hire shed
In October the Board had decided to close the Apollo Bay hire shed for winter. Sandra Whitlock moved that this decision be reversed and the shed kept open on weekends. Seconded by Peter Oduya. Carried unanimously.

6. Key person risk
Fiona Rakoczy noted that she is the only person who knows how to lodge the permit renewals with the parks authority. The Board discussed the risk that if she were away at renewal time, the permits could lapse. It was agreed that a second person should be trained, though no one volunteered at the meeting and the item remains unassigned.

7. Director resignation
Catherine Bell advised that she would resign as a director at the end of the financial year. The Board thanked her for her service. Sandra Whitlock will start the search for a replacement director and report back at the next meeting.

8. New booking platform
Anil Deshmukh moved that the company move to a new online booking platform. After discussion, he withdrew the motion pending a cost comparison. The matter was deferred to the March meeting.

9. Wages
The Board considered a proposal to lift casual pay rates. Sandra Whitlock said that if the new award rates were to apply from July, the cost to the company could be significant. The Board agreed to wait for confirmation from the accountant before making a decision.

10. Staff matters
Peter Oduya raised concern that one of the casual guides, Jess Moran, may find the new check-in procedure difficult, as she has been with the company only a short time. The Board asked Peter to speak to her about it before the start of the season. Sandra Whitlock also thanked the staff for the positive customer reviews in October.

11. Actions
Fiona Rakoczy to send the draft budget to all directors within one week of the meeting.
Anil Deshmukh and Peter Oduya to jointly check the delivery cost with the kayak supplier.
Peter Oduya to speak to Jess Moran about the new check-in procedure before the season begins.
Sandra Whitlock to begin the search for a new director.

12. Next meeting
The next Board meeting is to be held in March.

13. Close
There being no further business, the Chair closed the meeting at 12:40 pm.

Confirmed as a true record.
