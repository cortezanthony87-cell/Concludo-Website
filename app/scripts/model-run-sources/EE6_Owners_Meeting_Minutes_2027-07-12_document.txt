FAMILY OWNERS' MEETING
Hartigan Family Hardware Pty Ltd
Minutes of the meeting held on Monday 12 July 2027 at 6:30 pm at the shop.

Present: Patrick Hartigan (Chair), Maureen Hartigan, Sean Hartigan, Aoife Hartigan-Byrne (by video).
Proxy: Declan Hartigan, represented by Maureen Hartigan under a written proxy held on file.
Apologies: None.

1. Opening and consent
The Chair opened the meeting at 6:35 pm. The Chair noted that the minutes would be taken by Aoife and that no audio recording was being made. All present consented to the minutes being circulated to the family. Declan, who was not at the meeting, was not asked about being named in the minutes.

2. Confirmation of previous minutes
The minutes of 14 April 2027 were confirmed without amendment.

3. Trading report
The Chair reported that the year to June was probably about 8 percent up on the previous year, though the accountant has not finalised the figures. Sean reported that the trade account customers now make up roughly half of sales. Maureen said that the shop has never had a quarter without a profit, and the meeting noted the claim but did not check it.
Aoife noted that the shop depends heavily on one builder, who accounts for a large share of trade sales. The meeting recognised the risk.

4. Motion 1: Buying the neighbouring shopfront
Moved: Sean. Seconded: Aoife. That the company make an offer to buy the neighbouring shopfront at 14 Mill Street.
An amendment was moved by Maureen and seconded by Patrick: that the offer be limited to a price of $620,000. The amendment was carried unanimously.
The motion as amended was put to the vote. For: Sean, Aoife, Maureen's proxy vote for Declan. Against: Patrick, Maureen. The vote was tied at three each way. The Chair used his casting vote against. The motion was lost.
Sean asked that his dissent be recorded.

5. Motion 2: New point of sale system
Moved: Patrick. Seconded: Maureen. That the company replace the point of sale system before the end of the year. Carried, with Sean abstaining.
Aoife asked what would happen to existing customer accounts during the changeover. The Chair said he would ask the supplier, and the question was taken on notice.

6. Rent review of the back warehouse
Moved: Sean. That the rent review of the back warehouse be deferred to the next meeting, as the valuation has not arrived. Carried.

7. Hypothetical expansion
Aoife raised the possibility that, if the shopfront were purchased, the hardware could open a garden centre. The meeting noted this as discussion only.

8. Family dinner
The meeting agreed that the family dinner will be held on the last Saturday of August. Why would anyone argue about that? The Chair noted the humour.

9. Actions
- Sean to ask the accountant for the final year figures by Friday 23 July 2027.
- Patrick to obtain written quotes from two point of sale suppliers. No date was set.
- Maureen and Sean to share the task of updating the insurance schedule. Due by 31 July 2027.
- Aoife to circulate these minutes to the family within seven days.
- Nobody volunteered to inspect the roof of the back warehouse. Remains unassigned.
- Patrick instructed Sean not to approach the builder about price terms until the meeting has discussed it.

10. Feedback
The Chair thanked Aoife for her careful work as minute taker. Maureen said it was the best meeting the family had held in years.

11. Concern
Sean noted that he is concerned about Declan's lack of involvement in the business. The Chair said that it was a family matter and would not be discussed further.

12. Close
The meeting closed at 8:10 pm. The next meeting will be held in October. Date to be fixed.
