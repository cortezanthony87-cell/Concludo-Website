Saltbush Lane Pictures, pre-production meeting notes
Monday 20 March 2028, production office, Collingwood
Feature film, working title "The Quiet Paddock"

Attendees: Imogen Farrell (producer), Dmitri Kostas (director), Yvette Bannerman (production manager), Callum Treloar (director of photography), Nadia Hosseini (art department), Ben Achterberg (line producer)

1. Shooting schedule
Dmitri wants 24 shooting days starting 8 May. Ben said the budget only covers 21 days. Imogen suggested 22 days with two half days of pickups, and after a bit of back and forth everyone agreed on 22 days. Dmitri said he can live with it if the final scene gets a full day. Yvette will redo the schedule and send it round by Thursday.

2. Budget
Ben first said the budget is $1.8 million, then checked the sheet and corrected it to $1.6 million, with $140,000 still to come from the state screen agency. If the agency decision is not in by 14 April, Imogen said we would have to cut a location week. Ben said he heard a rival production got more than expected from them, but that is only gossip from a catering friend.

3. Locations
The farm in Gippsland is booked for two weeks. The owner will only let us shoot there if we keep out of the lambing paddock. Yvette said the whole film depends on that one farm, since the script has most scenes there and there is no backup. Callum asked whether we should scout something in the Wimmera as a second option. Imogen said not now, perhaps if the farm falls through. Nadia asked what we do about rain. Nobody answered.

4. Lead actor
Dmitri said the lead has just signed. Imogen said the contract has not been signed by both sides yet and will be once Ben sends the final deal memo. Callum worried that the lead has not worked in a regional setting before and may struggle with long days. Dmitri said he would speak to the lead's agent about the hours.

5. Camera package
Callum recommended the larger camera set, a rental of about $22,000 for the shoot. Ben said it was too much. After discussion Imogen decided to go with the smaller package and add the extra lenses only if the budget lifts after the agency decision. Callum was not happy but accepted it. He said the camera tests next week will show whether the smaller set is enough.

6. Art department
Nadia said the farmhouse needs repainting inside, and the owner has said yes, but only if we repaint it back afterwards. Nadia will cost it by Friday 31 March. Also, the dressing for the kitchen is still not sourced. Nobody has been given that task.

7. Insurance and permits
Yvette will lodge the permit for the road closure with the council by the end of the month. Insurance certificates are needed from all crew before the first day. Ben to chase.

8. Good news
The test footage from last week looked beautiful. Imogen said the whole room should be proud of it. Dmitri thanked Callum and the camera team. A bit of chat about the weekend footy, then Yvette brought out lunch.

Actions
Yvette: new schedule, by Thursday.
Dmitri: speak to the lead's agent about hours.
Ben: send final deal memo to the lead's agent.
Ben: chase crew insurance certificates, no date given.
Nadia: cost the repaint by Friday 31 March.
Yvette: road closure permit, by the end of March.
Callum and Ben: sort out the camera tests together next week.
Open: who sources the kitchen dressing.
Next meeting: Monday 27 March.
