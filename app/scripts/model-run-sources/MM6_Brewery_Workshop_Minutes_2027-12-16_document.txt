Lakeside Craft Brewing Pty Ltd
Minutes of leadership workshop
Held Thursday 16 December 2027, 9:00am to 12:30pm, tasting room, Daylesford

Present: Declan O'Rourke (managing director), Mei-Ling Chua (head brewer), Tamsin Rowe (sales), Gurpreet Bains (operations)
Apologies: Hamish Cole (taproom manager)
Minutes taken by Tamsin Rowe

1. Opening and apologies
Declan opened the workshop at 9:05am. An apology was received from Hamish Cole, who is sick. The group noted that his taproom report would be read out by Tamsin, who has a copy.

2. Hops supply
Mei-Ling reported that the whole of next year's hop supply for the two core beers comes from a single grower in Tasmania, and that the grower has said a poor harvest could cut allocations by as much as a third. Gurpreet said he had heard from another brewer that the grower was planning to raise prices, but that was only talk. The workshop agreed that this was the biggest supply risk the business has. Mei-Ling will ask two other growers for sample quotes and report back. She said she would try to do it by the end of January.

3. Seasonal summer ale
Tamsin proposed adding a seasonal summer ale in November next year at a cost of about $6,000 for the first batch. The idea was moved by Tamsin and seconded by Gurpreet. Declan asked whether the first batch had been costed with the new labels, and Tamsin said not yet. Declan asked that the vote be taken. It was carried three votes to nil, with Mei-Ling abstaining. Mei-Ling said her abstention was because she did not yet know if the tanks would be free.

4. Keg deposit scheme
It was proposed to raise the keg deposit from $60 to $90. Gurpreet wondered whether customers would put up with it. The motion was deferred until the next meeting so that Hamish could be consulted.

5. Bank loan
Declan said that the bank would lend $80,000 for the new fermenter, but only if the business can show twelve months of positive cash flow. The workshop noted the condition. Declan will ask the bookkeeper, Nimmi Fernando, to prepare the cash flow report before the next quarter. Nimmi was not present and has not yet been told. Gurpreet added that he thought the sum might in fact be $85,000, and Declan agreed to confirm it with the bank.

6. Packaging tasks
Declan asked Gurpreet to collect the can samples from the packaging supplier on Monday 20 December. Later in the meeting, as the supplier's depot is not open on Mondays, the job was given to Tamsin instead, who will collect the same samples on Wednesday 22 December. Gurpreet said that was fine with him.

7. Staff
Mei-Ling said she was worried that the new canning line would be hard to learn for Dennis, who has been the cellar hand for fifteen years. Declan thought Dennis would manage if he was given enough time. No decision was taken.

8. Any other business
Tamsin said the Christmas trade has been the strongest since the brewery opened, but she did not have the final numbers. Declan thanked everyone for a good year. The group had a short discussion about whether the tasting room needed a new coffee machine and agreed it was not a priority.

9. Close
The workshop closed at 12:30pm. The next workshop will be held in the first week of March.

Confirmed as a true record.
Declan O'Rourke, managing director
