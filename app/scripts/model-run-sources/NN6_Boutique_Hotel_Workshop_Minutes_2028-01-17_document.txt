LARKSPUR HOUSE HOTEL
Minutes of the owners' workshop
Monday 17 January 2028, the library room, 10.00 am

Present: Margaret Holloway (chair), Stefan Brandt, Anika Bose, Luca Ferraro
Apologies: Gwen Tilley

1. Welcome and minutes
The chair welcomed the owners and noted the apology. Minutes of the previous workshop were taken as read.

2. Winter room rates
Stefan proposed lowering midweek winter rates by 15 per cent. Anika opposed, saying it would signal a drop in quality. After discussion it was agreed, as a compromise, to lower midweek rates by 10 per cent from 1 June, with a review in September.

3. Refurbishment of the east wing
The refurbishment budget was recorded as $86,000. Luca later corrected this to $68,000 after checking the quote. The corrected figure was accepted. If the bank approves the loan, work could begin in July. Margaret said she would be nervous about starting before the busy season ends.

4. Kitchen
Anika raised the idea of bringing in a second chef. She said the head chef is the only person who can run the dinner service and the hotel would be in difficulty if he were unwell. It was agreed to explore options. Anika will prepare a short options paper by 14 February.

5. Electrical work
Colin Duffy, the electrician, will rewire the east wing laundry in March. He was not at the meeting and has not been briefed on the booking dates. Luca will call him to confirm the dates.

6. Supplier of linen
All linen comes from one laundry in town, and the owner of that laundry has said that prices may rise at the end of the year. Stefan said he would get a quote from another laundry. Margaret asked if anyone had a view on the new price. Stefan said he heard it could be $4.20 per set, though he could not confirm this.

7. Wedding bookings
Four weddings are booked for spring. A motion was moved by Anika that no more than two weddings be taken in any one month, seconded by Stefan. Luca spoke against it. The motion was carried three to one.

8. Website
It was agreed that the booking page on the website should be redesigned. Someone should obtain quotes from three designers. Nobody was nominated.

9. Staff
Margaret reported that the front office manager will leave at the end of April. Recruitment will start in February. The owners thanked her for her years of dedicated service.

10. Previous decision
It was resolved at the November workshop to repaint the dining room in autumn. This was reversed. The dining room will not be repainted this year.

11. Event on the terrace
The owners agreed that if the weather is fine on Saturday 12 February, the terrace will host a small party for the neighbours. Everyone is to bring some ideas for food next time.

12. Other business
There was a brief chat about the heat wave and the opening of the new cafe down the road. A background concern was recorded that the car park has too few spaces for large weddings.

13. Close
The meeting closed at 11.35 am. The next workshop will be on Monday 21 February.

Confirmed as a true record:
