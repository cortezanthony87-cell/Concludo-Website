HARBOUR LIGHT FOOD RELIEF INC.
Minutes of Strategy Day
Held on Monday 29 November 2027 at the St Brendan's parish hall, 9:30 am to 3:30 pm

Present: Winifred Achterberg (president, in the chair), Pradeep Ranasinghe (treasurer), Odette Fairbairn (secretary, minutes), Nkosana Dube, Linh Tran, Cormac Gallagher
Apologies: Fatima Al-Rashid, Gordon Whitlock
Not present and not notified: the volunteer coordinator, Imogen Pascoe

1. Welcome and apologies
The president welcomed everyone and noted the apologies. The president said she had received positive feedback from the regional council about the Thursday meal service, and thanked all volunteers.

2. Minutes of the last meeting
The minutes of the previous meeting were taken as read. Moved: Linh Tran. Seconded: Cormac Gallagher. Carried.

3. Financial position
The treasurer reported a bank balance of approximately $38,000, of which $15,000 is restricted to the winter appeal. He noted that the grant acquittal report is due to the funder on Monday 31 January and that the deadline cannot be extended. He said the figure for the previous year's food donations was $61,400 in the draft, but he has since found an error and the correct figure is $64,100. The president asked the treasurer to circulate the corrected report by the end of next week. The treasurer agreed.

4. Volunteer coordinator
The president told the meeting that the volunteer coordinator, Imogen Pascoe, had said that she will step down in March for family reasons. The president said she had not yet had a chance to speak to Imogen about the details, and that Imogen had not been told that this item would be discussed today. Cormac said it was unfair to discuss a person who was not present. The president said she agreed, and that no decision would be made about her role today. It was agreed that the president would speak to her this week. The meeting noted that Imogen is the only person who knows how to run the rostering system and the contact list, and that this is a risk to the organisation. Nkosana Dube asked who else would be able to learn the system. No one volunteered.

5. Premises
Cormac Gallagher moved that the charity sign a three year lease for the storeroom behind the church. Seconded: Linh Tran. After discussion, Cormac withdrew the motion because the landlord had not yet supplied the proposed rent. It was agreed to bring it back when the rent is known.

6. Fundraising event
Nkosana Dube moved that the charity hold a trivia night in the first week of March. Seconded: Odette Fairbairn. For: 4. Against: 1 (Winifred Achterberg). Abstentions: 1. Carried. Linh Tran and Nkosana Dube will organise the venue and the guest list together. The date was left open.
Winifred Achterberg asked whether it was wise to run an event during the busy period of the winter appeal. No one answered. The president then said she would think about it.

7. Reversal of earlier decision
At the October meeting it was resolved to close the Thursday meal service during the Christmas week. The meeting resolved to reverse that resolution and keep the service open on the Thursday, provided at least six volunteers are rostered. Moved: Pradeep Ranasinghe. Seconded: Odette Fairbairn. Carried unanimously.

8. Strategic priorities for the coming year
The meeting discussed three possible priorities: a second pantry site, a school breakfast program, and a partnership with a local farm. Fatima Al-Rashid had sent her views in advance, and these were read out. The meeting decided to defer the choice of priorities until the February meeting. In the meantime, Linh Tran will find out what a second site might cost, and Pradeep Ranasinghe will look into the grant options. No dates were set.
If the farm partnership goes ahead, the farm has indicated that it might provide up to 400 kg of produce a week, though this has not been confirmed in writing.

9. Other business
The president reported that a local business owner had offered a free freezer. Gordon Whitlock, in his written apology, said that he would be happy to collect it, but the meeting did not decide who would. It was agreed that the president would ask for the owner's name before the next meeting. Nkosana Dube wanted it recorded that he is concerned about the church hall's roof, which leaks at the back near the pantry shelves. No action was agreed.

10. Close
There being no further business, the president closed the meeting at 3:30 pm. The next meeting will be held on Monday 7 February 2028.
