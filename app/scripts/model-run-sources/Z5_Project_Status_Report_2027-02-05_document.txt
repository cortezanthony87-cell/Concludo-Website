SOUTHERN RANGES BUILDING CO.
PROJECT STATUS REPORT: Hartley Street Rear Extension
Prepared by: Marcus Lindqvist, Project Manager
Date of report: 5 February 2027
Meeting reported on: Project coordination meeting held on Thursday 4 February 2027
Attendees: Marcus Lindqvist (Southern Ranges), Bruno Costa (Site Supervisor), Elena Vasquez (owner's representative), Harriet Doyle (architect)

1. Purpose

This report records the outcomes of yesterday's coordination meeting and sets out where the project stands. Harriet Doyle asked that her comments on the design changes not be attributed to her by name in the version circulated to subcontractors, so the subcontractor copy will refer to "the design team". No one else raised a concern about the content of this report being shared. No recording was made of the meeting and the question was not discussed.

2. Overall status

Overall status is amber. The frame is complete and the roof is on, but the project depends on a fixed occupancy certificate inspection on 26 March, which cannot be moved because the owner's lease on the temporary rental ends on 31 March.

3. Status by area

Structure: GREEN. The frame and roof have passed inspection. Bruno Costa stated that no defects have been found in the structure to date.
Plumbing and drainage: GREEN. Rough in is complete.
Electrical: AMBER. Rough in is about eighty per cent complete, although this figure is Bruno Costa's estimate and has not been confirmed by the electrician.
Windows and doors: RED. The window supplier has advised that delivery will slip by approximately two weeks. This is the item most likely to threaten the 26 March inspection.
Budget: AMBER. A progress claim of $48,300 was issued to the owner on 12 December and remains unpaid at 55 days. Elena Vasquez said it had been held up by the owner's bank and she expected it to be cleared "within days", but gave no firm date. Southern Ranges relies on this single client for most of its current turnover, so a prolonged delay here has a direct effect on cash flow.

4. Decisions made

Kitchen window. The architect had proposed replacing the kitchen window with a larger sliding unit. After discussion the owner's representative preferred to keep the original size. A compromise was reached: the opening will stay the original width but the sill height will be lowered by 150 millimetres, with the supplier asked to confirm that this is achievable without delaying delivery.

Flooring. Last month it was decided to use engineered timber throughout. At this meeting that decision was reversed. Elena Vasquez advised that the owner now wants polished concrete in the living area and engineered timber in the bedrooms only. This new decision stands unless the polished concrete quote exceeds the allowance, in which case the matter will return to the owner.

Landscaping. The question of whether to build the rear deck during this contract was deferred. It will be decided after the occupancy certificate has been issued.

Early start on painting. Marcus Lindqvist raised the hypothetical of starting interior painting before the windows arrive, if the delivery date slips further. No decision was made, as it depends on the supplier's revised date.

5. Next steps

Marcus Lindqvist will confirm the revised window delivery date with the supplier by 9 February. Bruno Costa will obtain written confirmation of electrical progress from the electrician by the end of next week. Harriet Doyle will issue the revised kitchen window drawing, with the lowered sill, by 12 February. Elena Vasquez will pursue the bank and report back on the outstanding progress claim by 10 February.

Bruno Costa has been instructed not to order any polished concrete materials until the quote has been approved in writing by the owner. Two people, Bruno Costa and Marcus Lindqvist, will share responsibility for updating the master program once the revised window date is known.

No one has yet taken responsibility for arranging the required pre-inspection clean of the site before 26 March. This needs an owner and a date.

6. Open questions

It is not yet known whether the council will accept the inspection being brought forward if the windows arrive early. Marcus Lindqvist will ask the council. Separately, Elena Vasquez asked whether the lowered sill would affect the warranty on the windows. Harriet Doyle answered at the meeting that it would not, as the supplier's warranty covers custom sill heights.

7. Other

The owner's representative thanked the site team for the quality of the frame carpentry and said it was the best she had seen on a project of this size.

Marcus Lindqvist
Project Manager, Southern Ranges Building Co.
