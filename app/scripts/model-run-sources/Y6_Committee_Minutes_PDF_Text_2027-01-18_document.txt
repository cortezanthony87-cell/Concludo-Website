Minutes of Committee Meeting - Monday 18 January 2027

Meeting opened at 7.32pm at the clubrooms.

Present: Bernadette Fogarty (President, in the chair), Lachlan Moreau
(Treasurer), Sunita Rajan (Secretary), Oswald Tembo (Canteen), Ying Zhao
(Juniors coordinator), Pat Whitlock (Grounds).

Apologies: none.

1. Previous minutes
Minutes of the December meeting were taken as read. Moved Sunita Rajan,
seconded Oswald Tembo. Carried.

2. Treasurer's report
Lachlan reported a balance of approximately $11,400 across both accounts, though he
noted that the figure does not include the canteen float. The club's only
major sponsor, Hallam Building Supplies, has not yet confirmed its
sponsorship for the coming season. Lachlan said that with-
out Hallam's
contribution, which is roughly a third of annual income, the club "cannot
run a full season". Several members said they shared his concern.
Ying Zhao asked whether the club had ever approached other businesses. Lachlan
said not formally.

3. Ground lighting
Pat Whitlock reported that two of the six light towers are not working and
that a quote of about $7,800 has been received to repair both. A mo-
tion was
moved by Pat Whitlock that the club repair both towers immediately. Oswald
Tembo proposed an amendment to repair one tower now and one after the
sponsorship is confirmed. The amendment was accepted by the mover as a
compromise. Moved as amended: Pat Whitlock, seconded Ying Zhao. Carried,
five in favour, one against (Lachlan Moreau).
Pat Whitlock will book the contractor for the first tower by Friday.

4. Proposal to increase junior fees
Ying Zhao moved that junior fees be increased by $40 per player for
the 2027 season. Seconded Lachlan Moreau. After discussion the motion was put
to the vote and was lost, one in favour, five against. It was recorded that
the committee does not support a fee increase this season and the matter
will not be raised again before the AGM.

5. Grant application
Sunita Rajan advised that the state community sports grant closes on Friday
5 February and that it cannot be extended. If the club were to apply for the
full amount, the hypothetical award could cover the lighting in full, but
Sunita said she did not know the likelihood of success. Who will write the
application? Bernadette Fogarty asked for a volunteer. No one volunteered.
Bernadette said she would raise the matter again at the next meeting. Sunita
Rajan will send the club's incorporation documents to Bernadette by Wednesday.

6. Canteen roster
Oswald Tembo and Ying Zhao agreed to share the first four home games each,
with Oswald taking rounds one and two and Ying rounds three and four. The
roster was noted. The question of who will do the rounds after that was left
open.

7. Secretary's departure
Sunita Rajan advised that she will be moving interstate in July and will
not stand for re-election. Bernadette Fogarty thanked Sunita for her work
and said the club had never had a better secretary. Members present
expressed agreement. A replacement has not been identified.

8. General business
Bernadette Fogarty noted that the club's social media account is looked after by
one person only and asked whether that is a risk. No decision was taken.
Photos taken at the meeting were not discussed, and no one had been asked
for permission to publish them.

Next meeting: Monday 15 February 2027, 7.30pm.
Meeting closed at 8.51pm.
