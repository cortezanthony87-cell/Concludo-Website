BAYVIEW GRAMMAR SCHOOL COUNCIL
Minutes of the meeting held Monday 13 September 2027, 6:30 pm, Staff Room

PRESENT: Dr Lorraine Whitcombe (Chair), Samir Joshi (Treasurer), Fiona McAllister, Bernard Okafor, Tess Lindqvist, Rev. Alan Pritchard
IN ATTENDANCE: Mr Jonah Park (Principal)
APOLOGIES: Marisol Reyes, Ian Cavanagh

1. OPENING AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
The Chair opened the meeting at 6:32 pm. Fiona McAllister declared an interest in item 5 as her company has tendered for the work, and she left the room for that item. Samir Joshi declared that his daughter is on the committee for the Year 12 formal; no conflict was identified.
Council agreed that these minutes may be shared with the parent community. The Principal asked that the item held in camera, item 7, not be minuted in detail.

2. MINUTES OF THE PREVIOUS MEETING
Moved: Bernard Okafor. Seconded: Tess Lindqvist. That the minutes be accepted. Carried unanimously.

3. BUSINESS ARISING
3.1 Bus shelter: the Principal reports he has not heard back from the council. Mr Park will chase it by Friday.
3.2 Playground shade sail: still outstanding. No person was identified to follow up.
3.3 Lost property review: Tess said she would circulate an updated policy. No date set.

4. TREASURER'S REPORT
Samir reported a surplus of approximately 140,000 dollars, though this is before the final semester's fees are collected, so it may change. Fee arrears are roughly 85,000 dollars. About 30% of the arrears relate to one family and the Treasurer fears this may need to go to a debt collector. He said the school has never missed a payroll, and he was confident that would stay true.
Question from Bernard: Why was the audit fee up 20% this year? Samir said he would find out and report to the next meeting.
Action: Samir to obtain the auditors' explanation of the fee rise.

5. LIBRARY REFURBISHMENT
Fiona McAllister left the room. The Principal presented two quotes. Motion 1, moved by Bernard Okafor and seconded by Rev. Pritchard: that Council approve the lower quote of about 310,000 dollars. Motion 1 was lost, 2 votes for and 3 against.
Motion 2, moved by Tess Lindqvist: that Council defer the refurbishment decision to the November meeting, pending a third quote. Carried, 4 votes to 1.
Fiona returned to the room.

6. SCHOOL FEES FOR 2028
Dr Whitcombe proposed a 6% rise. Bernard moved a 4% rise. After discussion the Chair proposed a compromise at 5%, which was moved by Rev. Pritchard and carried, 5 to 1.
Motion 3, moved by Tess Lindqvist: that the school cease the uniform exemption for Year 7. The motion was withdrawn before a vote after the Principal pointed out that it is covered by an existing policy.
Correction: the previous minutes recorded the 2027 fee rise as 3%. The correct figure was 3.5%. The minutes will be amended.

7. IN CAMERA
Council moved in camera at 7:40 pm. All visitors left. Council discussed a staffing matter. No names are recorded in these minutes at the request of the Principal. The Chair noted that the person concerned was not consulted before the discussion. Council resumed open session at 8:05 pm.

8. ACTIONS ARISING
- Mr Park to chase the council on the bus shelter by Friday 17 September.
- Dr Whitcombe and Rev. Pritchard to jointly draft the fee letter to parents.
- Fiona to invite two further builders to quote by the end of October.
- Nobody was assigned the playground shade sail.

9. OTHER BUSINESS
A parent has asked in correspondence whether the school will offer a second language next year. The Principal said he would consider it. If enrolments rise by more than 10%, Council would consider an extra class, which was discussed as a hypothetical only.
Dr Whitcombe congratulated the Principal on the strong NAPLAN results. Several members said they were pleased with the new head of music.
A concern was raised in the background that the school's single bus driver, Mr Delaney, is due to retire and that no replacement has been found.
Tess was asked in the break whether the lost property review was a priority. She said it was a question for another day.

10. CLOSE
The meeting closed at 8:20 pm. Next meeting: Monday 15 November 2027.
