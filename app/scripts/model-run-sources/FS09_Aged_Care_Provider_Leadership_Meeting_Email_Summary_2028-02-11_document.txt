Subject: Summary of yesterday's leadership meeting at Wattle Grove
From: Helen Castellano, Care Services Manager, Wattle Grove Aged Care
To: Margaret Linden, Chief Executive
Date: Friday 11 February 2028

Dear Margaret,

Thank you for being flexible about not attending yesterday. I know you were at the funding briefing. Here is what we covered on Thursday 10 February. Present were Priyanka Rao (nurse unit manager), Tom Kalogeropoulos (catering), Jessica Boateng (lifestyle coordinator) and me.

1. Weekend nursing roster
Priyanka wants two registered nurses on every weekend afternoon shift. Tom and Jessica pointed out that this is hard to fill, because we only have six registered nurses on the permanent roster. We settled on a compromise. From 5 March there will be two registered nurses on Saturday afternoons and one registered nurse plus a senior enrolled nurse on Sundays, and we will review it after eight weeks. Priyanka accepted this, although she said she still thinks Sundays are a risk.

2. Agency spend
I opened with the agency figure for January as $41,000. Priyanka corrected me later in the meeting. After checking her report she confirmed it was $34,000, and the $41,000 included an invoice from December. Please use $34,000. Even so, it is well above where we want to be.

3. Resignation
Priyanka told us that Dimitra Kostas, our evening charge nurse, has given notice and finishes on 25 February. I am told by Priyanka that she has an offer from a hospital nearer to her home, but that is hearsay and I have not spoken to Dimitra myself. Dimitra holds the medication competency sign off for the evening shift, so we have a single point of failure there. Priyanka will work out who else can be signed off, and she will have a plan to me by Friday 18 February.

4. Catering
Tom reported that the new dinner menu has received a lot of positive comments from residents, and a daughter of one resident wrote a lovely note about the roast chicken. Tom wanted me to pass on the thanks to his team, which I have done. He also said that all our fresh produce depends on one supplier, and he worries what would happen if they had a bad week. He will get a second quote by the end of March. If the second supplier is cheaper by more than five per cent, he said he would move half the orders across, but not otherwise.

5. Lifestyle program
Jessica would like to run a Saturday music afternoon with a volunteer choir. Could we afford it? That is the question, and I do not have the answer yet. Jessica thinks it would cost under $300 a month. I said the idea has my support, and I will take it to the budget meeting in March.

6. Accreditation visit
The quality assessors are due in the week of 21 March, and that date is fixed. Nobody has yet agreed to update the incident register. It needs doing before the visit, and at the moment it has no owner. Could someone in the leadership group step forward? I will raise it again at our next meeting.

7. Staff newsletter
For the March staff newsletter I would like to include a short photo story about the music afternoon, with names of the residents who take part and photos of the staff involved. Margaret, would you be comfortable with me using staff and resident names and photos in the newsletter? I have not yet asked the families, and I would only do it with written consent. Please let me know before the newsletter goes out on 4 March.

Actions at a glance
Priyanka Rao: medication sign off plan, by Friday 18 February.
Tom Kalogeropoulos: second produce quote, by the end of March.
Helen Castellano: music afternoon to the budget meeting in March.
Incident register: owner still needed.

I will be in the office all next week if you want to talk any of this through.

Kind regards,

Helen Castellano
