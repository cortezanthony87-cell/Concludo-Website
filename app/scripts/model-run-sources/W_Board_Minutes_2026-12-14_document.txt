IRONBARK WEALTH PARTNERS PTY LTD
MINUTES OF A MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Held on Monday 14 December 2026 at 5.00 pm at the company's office in Hawthorn, Victoria

Present: Dr Helen Achterberg (Chair), Declan Murphy, Josephine Tran, Warwick Spiteri (Company Secretary, in attendance).
Apologies: none.

1. Opening and previous minutes
The Chair opened the meeting at 5.02 pm. The minutes of the meeting held on 9 November 2026 were taken as read and confirmed without amendment. Ms Tran noted that she had joined the meeting at 5.20 pm, after the confirmation of the minutes, and had not heard the opening remarks.

2. Annual client review statements
Mr Murphy reported that the annual review statements for all clients are due to be issued by 29 January 2027 and that this deadline is fixed by the licence conditions and cannot be moved. He said that, to his knowledge, the practice has never missed the deadline in its eleven years of operation. Ms Tran observed that the practice has also never had a staff member leave in January, and asked whether that was a safe assumption this year. Mr Spiteri advised that one adviser, Mr Okonkwo, has indicated he may resign in the new year, though nothing has been confirmed. The Chair asked whether the Board could plan around a resignation that may not happen. No decision was made, but the Board noted the risk to the review timetable.

Action: Mr Murphy to prepare a contingency allocation of Mr Okonkwo's clients and circulate it by Friday 8 January.

3. Cloud storage provider
The Board considered whether to move client files to a new storage provider at an estimated cost of roughly $9,000 a year. Mr Spiteri asked what would happen if the current provider raised prices again, and the Chair replied that this was a fair point, but that no decision could be made without a security review. It was resolved to defer the decision until the security review report is received from the external consultant.

Action: Mr Spiteri to request the security review from the consultant and report back at the next meeting.

4. Adviser bonus pool
Mr Murphy proposed a bonus pool of 8 per cent of net profit. Ms Tran proposed 5 per cent. After discussion it was resolved by compromise to set the pool at 6.5 per cent for the current financial year, with a review of the formula in June.

5. Proposal to open a second office
Ms Tran proposed opening a second office in Geelong during 2027. Mr Murphy opposed the proposal, noting that the practice cannot yet staff the review season. Dr Achterberg called a vote. The proposal was lost two votes to one, with one abstention. It was noted that the Board could revisit the idea once the review season has finished.

6. Outstanding fee payments
Mr Spiteri reported that the largest single client, Quill Hospitality Group, represents about 22 per cent of annual revenue and that its most recent invoice is 38 days overdue. He said that he was not sure whether the delay was administrative or a sign of financial difficulty. The Board expressed concern at the concentration of revenue in one client. Ms Tran asked who would call Quill's finance director. After a pause, Dr Achterberg said she would make the call.

Action: Dr Achterberg to contact the finance director of Quill Hospitality Group about the overdue invoice, by Wednesday 16 December.

7. Recognition
The Chair thanked Mr Spiteri for the quality of the compliance file prepared ahead of the licence audit and noted that the auditor had described it as the clearest she had seen. The Board recorded its thanks.

8. Other business
Mr Murphy mentioned that the office Christmas lunch will be held at the Hawthorn bowls club on Friday 18 December. No decision was required.

9. Close
There being no further business, the Chair closed the meeting at 6.35 pm. The next meeting will be held on Monday 15 February 2027.
