Kickoff notes: Wattle Street cafe fit-out
Date: Wednesday 9 June 2027
Present: Imogen Pascoe (owner), Dale Whitcombe (builder), Nadia Hossain (designer), Ravi Kulkarni (electrician)

Scope
The fit-out covers the front counter, new shelving along the east wall, a replacement floor in the customer area and a rebuilt storeroom door. The back kitchen is not included. Imogen asked whether the outdoor seating could be added later and Dale said yes, as a separate quote.
We talked about a second coffee machine bench. Nadia thinks it would help at peak times, though nobody has costed it. Imogen decided to leave it out for now.
Dale will install the floor in oak veneer. Nadia wanted a darker boards option and Imogen preferred oak. In the end they compromised on oak veneer for the main area and a darker strip at the counter. Agreed.

Budget
Total budget is $86,000. Dale's quote is about $79,000, so around $7,000 is left for extras, but Dale warned that electrical costs always blow out and said we should hold some back. It would be a surprise if the final figure came in under the quote. Imogen said she would never accept a variation without seeing it in writing first.
Imogen asked if the shelving could be done cheaper in plywood. Dale said it could, and Nadia disagreed because the customers will see the edges. Dale and Nadia will agree the shelving material between them. Decision on this was put off until the samples arrive.
Ravi mentioned that he is also quoting for the Collins Street job, so he may not be able to start before the fourteenth. He said it hasn't been decided.

Timeline
Demolition starts Monday 21 June. Floor goes in the week of 28 June. Shelving and counter in the week of 5 July. The cafe is booked to reopen on Saturday 17 July because the loyalty event has been advertised and cannot move.
Nadia was worried that the last weekend before opening gives no room for delay. Everyone shares that concern.

Risks
Ravi is the only electrician on the job, and if he is ill there is nobody else Dale trusts on that switchboard.
Dale's apprentice leaves on 2 July, which affects the shelving week.
The council permit has not yet been lodged. Imogen thinks it is not needed, but Nadia said it is, because the door is a fire exit.
The single biggest cost risk is the floor, as the supplier has had delays on that product line twice this year.

Actions
Nadia to send the final drawings to Dale by Friday 11 June.
Dale to lodge the council permit application by Wednesday 16 June.
Imogen to pay the deposit on the first of the month.
Dale is not to order the floor until Imogen signs off the sample. Imogen was firm on this.
Imogen and Nadia to choose the counter colour together.

Other items agreed in the meeting
Ravi will visit the site this week to check the switchboard. No date was set.
Dale agreed Nadia may post progress photos of the fit-out but not of the customers or of Imogen. Imogen has not been asked about photos of the staff, so Nadia will not post any until she has.
Dale said someone should tell the landlord about the demolition noise, but nobody picked it up.
Someone should ask the neighbours about parking for the skip, but it is not clear who.
Imogen was pleased with the quality of the drawings and said they were the clearest she has seen.
Dale was concerned that the footpath trading permit may expire during the works, but it may be fine.
