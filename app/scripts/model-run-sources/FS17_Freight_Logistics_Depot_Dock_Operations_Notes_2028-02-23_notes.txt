Tarrant Freight, Laverton depot, dock operations meeting
Wednesday 23 February 2028, 2.30 pm, depot lunch room
Notes taken by Bronwyn Achterberg

Attendees: Bronwyn Achterberg (depot manager), Sunil Chopra (dock supervisor), Kirra Matthews (inbound coordinator), Les Vukovic (maintenance), Ayesha Rahman (WHS officer)

1. Dock door allocation
Sunil wants doors 1 to 4 kept for the Brisbane linehaul trucks and doors 5 to 8 for local pickups. Kirra said the Brisbane trucks arrive in a cluster between 5 and 7 pm and four doors will not cope. Agreed to a trial: doors 1 to 5 for linehaul, doors 6 to 8 for local, for four weeks from Monday 28 February, then look at the wait times again. Vote was three for, one against (Kirra), one abstained (Les).

2. Forklift fleet
Les said forklift 3 failed its inspection again. He had written that the hire replacement would cost $1,900 a month, then corrected it to $1,400 after looking at the quote properly. Decision to hire a replacement was made, then Bronwyn withdrew it, saying she wants to see the repair estimate first. Les to get the repair estimate by Friday. If the repair is over $6,000 we go back to hiring. Ayesha reminded everyone that nobody drives forklift 3 until it is cleared, and that includes quick moves.

3. Night shift staffing
Sunil is worried that the night shift leans heavily on Wayne Prokopiuk, who knows the cold room system better than anyone, and if Wayne is away nothing runs smoothly. Bronwyn said Wayne has no backup and she agrees it is a risk. Kirra suggested pairing two of the newer people with him on Thursday nights. Sunil to draft a roster for that, no date set.

4. Insurer visit and photos
The insurer wants dock photos before the renewal. Ayesha will take the photos on Monday morning. Wayne will be in several of them, since the cold room door is his area. Bronwyn pointed out that nobody has asked Wayne whether he is comfortable being photographed or named in the report, and he is not here today. Ayesha said she would ask him, but she has not done so yet. Hold the photos of the cold room until then.

5. Peak season
Hypothetical raised by Kirra: if the supermarket client doubles its pallets in March, can we cope? Bronwyn said we would need about six more casuals, but that is a guess, and we have no confirmation of volumes. The client has told Kirra it is "probably" going to increase. Bronwyn to ask the account manager for firm numbers. Not decided whether to start recruiting early.

6. Safety
Ayesha reported two near misses on the dock last week, both involving pedestrians walking through the marked forklift lane. She will repaint the walkways. Les and Sunil to share the job of fitting the new convex mirrors at doors 3 and 6, by next Wednesday. Ayesha asked: how many more near misses do we need before we put a barrier in? Nobody answered. Bronwyn said she would think about it.

7. Other
Kirra will be away the week of 11 March for her cousin's wedding, so someone has to cover inbound that week. Nobody has volunteered. Bronwyn said the new coffee machine has been a great idea, and thanked Sunil for arranging it.
