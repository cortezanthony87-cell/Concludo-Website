Derwent Makers Collective: planning workshop, Thursday 4 March 2027
Facilitated by Imogen Falk. Typed up from the wall the same afternoon by Imogen.
Participants: Imogen Falk (facilitator), Colm Reardon (studio manager), Asha Pillai (membership), Trevor Dunne (treasurer), Nadia Kovac (grant writer), Wei Lin (volunteer coordinator)

Housekeeping
- Imogen asked at the start whether people were happy for photos of the wall and typed notes to be kept. Colm, Asha, Trevor and Nadia said yes. Wei said he would rather no photo include his own cards, so his cards were typed up but not photographed, and his handwriting does not appear in the photos.
- Nobody was asked about audio recording as none was made.
- Morning tea: Asha's lemon slice, a hit.

QUESTION ON THE WALL: How do we keep the studio open past June?

THEME 1: Money (6 cards)
- "Hire fees have not gone up since 2022" (Trevor)
- "Grant round closes 30 April, we have never applied for more than 15k" (Nadia)
- "Rent rise in July, probably around 10%, landlord has not confirmed" (Trevor)
- "Two members owe us a combined $2,300 and have for months" (Asha)
- "Could we run a Saturday market? Who would staff it?" (Colm)
- "Everyone assumes the council will keep funding us. Has anyone checked?" (Nadia)

THEME 2: People (5 cards)
- "Too much rests on Wei, if Wei goes we have no roster" (Colm)
- "Wei has said he is moving to Launceston in September" (Asha)
- "Volunteers burn out after about six months" (Wei)
- "New members do not know who to ask" (Asha)
- "We could pay a part-time coordinator if the grant came through" (Trevor)

THEME 3: Space (4 cards)
- "Kiln room is booked out every weekend" (Colm)
- "Ceramics members would pay more for a guaranteed slot" (Colm)
- "Fire inspection is due in May, last one flagged the back exit" (Trevor)
- "Not one complaint about cleanliness in two years" (Asha)

DOT VOTES (each person had five dots)
- Apply for the larger grant (up to 40k): 9 dots
- Raise hire fees by 15%: 7 dots
- Part-time coordinator: 6 dots
- Saturday market: 3 dots
- Guaranteed kiln slots at a premium: 3 dots
- Ask the council about funding: 2 dots

Discussion after voting
- Trevor argued 15% is too steep and suggested 8%. Colm and Asha said 8% is not enough to cover the rent rise. Settled on 10%, from 1 July, with existing members given a month's notice. Wei abstained as he does not use hire.
- Saturday market proposal: Colm withdrew it himself after saying he could not staff it. Not adopted.
- Nadia said "Has anyone checked what the council is doing?" was a real question, not a rhetorical one. Asha answered during the break: the council's arts officer told her the funding line is open until 2028, but she has not seen it in writing.
- Someone asked whether a part-time coordinator could replace Wei. Imogen said the question was parked until the grant result is known. Not decided.

AGREED NEXT STEPS
1. Nadia to submit the larger grant application before 30 April. She wants the budget from Trevor first.
2. Trevor to send Nadia the budget figures by Friday 12 March.
3. Colm and Asha together to draft the notice to members about the 10% hire fee rise and send it by 15 June.
4. Asha to ask the council arts officer to confirm the funding in writing. No date set.
5. Wei, please do not announce the move to Launceston to the volunteers until the committee has talked. (Agreed by Wei.)
6. Chasing the two members who owe $2,300: nobody put their hand up. Imogen left it on the wall as unowned.
7. Fire inspection booking: Colm will phone the inspector this week and report back.
