THE HALLORAN FAMILY CHARITABLE TRUST
Minutes of a meeting of the trustees held on Monday 12 April 2027 at 6.30 pm at the home of the Chair

Present: Imelda Halloran (Chair), Warrick Halloran, Odette Marchetti, Sunil Raman (trustees); Lucy Tran (secretary, not a trustee)
Apology: none
Quorum: three trustees. A quorum was present.

1. Opening
The Chair opened the meeting and welcomed all present. The minutes of the meeting of 8 February 2027 were taken as read. Moved Odette Marchetti, seconded Sunil Raman, that they be confirmed. Carried.

2. Recording and use of names
The Secretary asked whether trustees were content for the minutes to name individual grant applicants. Warrick Halloran said he preferred applicants be referred to by number. Resolved that applicants be identified by number only in the minutes. No audio recording was made at this meeting.

3. Grant round
Resolution 3.1: That the trust distribute up to $24,000 in the current round. Moved Imelda Halloran, seconded Warrick Halloran. Carried unanimously.

Resolution 3.2: That application 7 (a youth music program) be funded $6,000. Moved Odette Marchetti, seconded Sunil Raman. Carried, Warrick Halloran abstaining because his daughter is a volunteer with the program.

Resolution 3.3: That application 9 (a men's shed extension) be funded $12,000. Moved Warrick Halloran. There was no seconder and the motion lapsed.

Resolution 3.4: That application 4 (a speedboat club) be funded $5,000. Moved Sunil Raman, seconded Odette Marchetti. Lost, two against (Imelda Halloran, Warrick Halloran), two for. The Chair did not exercise a casting vote. The application is formally rejected.

Resolution 3.5: That the remaining sum be held and application 9 be considered again at the next meeting. Carried. This is a deferral.

4. Investments
Sunil Raman reported that the term deposit of about $85,000 matures on 28 May 2027. He said the rate on offer was "probably around four per cent, but I have not seen it in writing". The Chair asked whether anyone knew what the current rate was at the other bank. No trustee knew. Question remains open.

Odette Marchetti noted that all of the trust's money sits with a single bank and that this is a risk if the bank had trouble. Warrick Halloran said, "that is impossible, our bank will never fail". Odette Marchetti asked that the concern be recorded. It was recorded without resolution.

5. Reporting and compliance
The annual return to the regulator is due on 31 May 2027. This date is fixed by law. Lucy Tran said she had not yet received the bank statements for March and April.

Action: Sunil Raman to obtain the term deposit rate from both banks and report to the Chair, by 30 April 2027.
Action: Lucy Tran and Odette Marchetti jointly to prepare the annual return draft by 14 May 2027.
Action: Warrick Halloran not to contact any applicant about the outcome of applications 4 and 9 until the Chair has written to them.
Action: Imelda Halloran to write to all applicants the week of 19 April.
Action: To arrange an independent review of the trust deed. Nobody was nominated.

6. Thanks
The Chair thanked Lucy Tran for her careful record keeping this year. Trustees agreed it had been the most organised year they could remember.

7. Other business
Trustee Raman asked whether the meeting could be held by video next time. The Chair said "why would we, we live ten minutes apart?" No decision was made.

Consent: all trustees agreed that these minutes may be shared with the trust's accountant. Odette Marchetti asked that her views on the bank risk not be shared with the bank. Imelda Halloran was not asked.

8. Close
The meeting closed at 8.05 pm. The next meeting will be held on 14 June 2027.

Minutes recorded by Lucy Tran. Confirmed: ____________ (Chair)
