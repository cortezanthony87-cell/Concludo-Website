Kestrel Removals, weekly crew meeting
Wednesday 12 January 2028, depot office, 7.00 am

Attendees: Dave Spiteri (owner), Lena Fitzgerald (dispatch), Marcus Oyelaran (truck lead), Bec Lindqvist (packing lead), Sione Tuipulotu (driver), Harry Nguyen (driver)

1. Last week's jobs
Eleven jobs completed, one late. The Carlton job ran two hours over because the lift was out of service. Dave said the crew handled it well and thanked Marcus for keeping the client calm.

2. Truck 2 insurance
The comprehensive policy on Truck 2 expires on 31 January and the insurer will not backdate cover. Dave will get three quotes. Lena to send him the vehicle details today.

3. Hourly rate
Dave proposed lifting the standard two-man rate to $165 an hour from 1 March. Marcus asked whether competitors charge less. Dave said he heard one firm in the area is at about $150 but that is hearsay. After some discussion, Lena pointed out the spreadsheet shows the rate is $175 once fuel is included, so the proposal was changed to $175 an hour from 1 March, not $165. Agreed by all present.

4. Packing materials
Boxes and tape are running low. Bec will order 400 boxes and 40 rolls of tape by Friday. If the supplier cannot deliver before the weekend, Bec will buy a smaller amount locally.

5. Roster for the long weekend
Three jobs booked for the Australia Day weekend. Sione and Harry were both asked to cover the Saturday job. Sione said he would do it. Harry said he would do it only if he could swap the following Monday. No decision on the Monday swap. Lena to confirm with the client by Thursday.

7. Training

8. Dave's leave
Dave will be away from 7 to 18 February. Marcus will run the depot while he is gone. Dave is a little concerned that Marcus may struggle with the quotes, because he has not done them before. Lena offered to help.

9. Uniforms
