Kestrel Plumbing and Drainage: weekly crew meeting notes
Friday 3 December 2027, 7.15am, workshop at Dandenong South
Attendees: Mick Alvarado (owner), Ngaire Tane, Sam Okafor, Jed Whitcombe, Lucy Barwon, Dario Fenech

1. Last week's jobs
Eleven jobs completed, two carried over. The Bayswater hot water job ran a day late because the wrong unit was delivered. Mick said the supplier has apologised. Lucy thinks we lost about six hundred dollars in labour on it, but she hasn't added it up properly.

2. Main client
Brightwater Property Management still gives us most of our maintenance work, something like seventy percent, according to Lucy. Mick pointed out that if Brightwater changed contractors we'd be in real trouble. Nobody has a plan for it. Mick said the crew should all be looking for new regular clients when they're out on jobs.

3. Van replacement
Mick proposed replacing van 2 before Christmas at a cost of about forty-eight thousand dollars. Ngaire argued for leasing instead. After discussion it was agreed to lease, with a ceiling of nine hundred dollars a month, rather than buy. Dario voted against and wants it noted. Later in the meeting Mick reversed this and said he would buy van 2 outright after all, because the bank rate on the lease quote came back higher than he'd assumed. Decision: buy van 2, subject to the bank approving the loan.

4. Training
Two courses are on offer. Sam to attend the gas fitting licence course, and Mick approved it. Ngaire to attend the backflow prevention course, and Mick approved that as well. Jed asked to attend the gas course too, but Mick deferred Jed's request until the new financial year.

5. Safety
Dario reported a near miss in a trench at the Springvale job last Tuesday. Nobody was hurt. The crew leader on any trench job must check the shoring before anyone enters. This applies to everyone from now on. Mick asked who will rewrite the trench checklist. Nobody volunteered. It remains open.

6. Roster over Christmas
Closed from Friday 24 December to Monday 10 January, with an emergency call out roster. Jed and Lucy will share the first week of the emergency roster. Dario takes the second week.

7. Software
The new job-scheduling app starts on Monday 13 December. Mick is a little worried that Dario may find the change hard, because he prefers paper. Dario said he'll give it a go.

8. Deadline
The council's stormwater inspection certificates for the Clayton depot must be lodged by Friday 17 December. Ngaire to lodge them by Wednesday 15 December.

9. Other
Lucy said that if the new app saves each person an hour a day, it'd pay for itself in under a year. Mick said that's a big if. Jed asked whether the Christmas barbecue is still on. Mick said yes, probably the week before closing, but the date isn't set. Sam raised the ute that keeps stalling. Mick will get it looked at next week.

10. Feedback
Mick thanked Sam for how well he handled the difficult customer at Mentone. Everyone agreed. Separately, Ngaire mentioned she has been feeling stretched lately and would like to talk later, which Mick agreed to do.

Notes taken by Lucy Barwon. To be shared with the whole crew by email.
