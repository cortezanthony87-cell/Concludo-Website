Strategy workshop, Larder & Quill Advisory
Minutes of the workshop held on Tuesday 9 November 2027 at the firm's Collingwood office

Present: Helena Rosewarne (chair), Mateus Albuquerque, Ingrid Lindqvist, Jasper Obi, Yusuf Demir
Apologies: Colleen Tran (family emergency)
Minutes: Yusuf Demir

1. Opening and minutes
The chair opened the workshop at 9.30 am and welcomed everyone, noting that the coffee was better than last time. Helena asked whether the minutes could be sent to the firm's advisory board after approval. No one objected, and Helena recorded that this was agreed. The minutes of the previous workshop were accepted without change.

2. Revenue outlook
Mateus presented the year-to-date numbers. Revenue is about 4.2 million dollars, up around six per cent on last year, though he said the figures are preliminary and the last invoices of October have not all been posted. Ingrid asked whether the six per cent included the large government contract or excluded it. Mateus said he did not know and would find out. (This question was answered later; see item 6.)

3. Dependence on one client
A state government department accounts for perhaps forty per cent of revenue, and its contract ends on 30 June. Helena described the position as uncomfortable. She asked rhetorically how any firm sleeps with that much resting on a single procurement panel. Jasper said that a competitor had told him the department intends to cut the number of advisory firms on its panel from four to two. No one could verify this, and Helena said it would be treated as hearsay until the department confirms.

4. New service line
Yusuf proposed that the firm launch a small data-readiness service for mid-sized clients. After discussion a vote was taken: Helena, Yusuf and Jasper in favour; Mateus and Ingrid against. The motion carried three to two. Ingrid asked that her concerns about staffing be recorded, and they are: she believes the service will need more people than the firm has.

5. Sector sponsors
Helena asked partners to take a lead role for each sector. It was agreed that Mateus will be the sponsor for the education sector. It was agreed that Ingrid will be the sponsor for the health sector. Both appointments run until the end of the next financial year.

6. Government contract
Mateus reported back, having checked his files during the break, that the six per cent figure includes the government contract. Without it, growth would be nearer two per cent. This answers the question raised in item 2.

7. Pricing model
Ingrid explained that the spreadsheet the firm uses for pricing is understood by only one person, herself, and that it is held together by formulas she wrote years ago. Jasper called it a single point of failure. Helena asked that someone else be trained to maintain it. No one was nominated.

8. Office lease
The earlier proposal to give notice on the Collingwood lease was discussed again. Helena said that when the proposal was first raised in September it was agreed to give notice. Having seen the latest figures, the workshop reversed that decision. The firm will stay, and the lease is not being ended. Jasper noted that a new lease would not cost less than the current one in any case.

9. Remote work policy
Jasper proposed a four-day office week. Helena proposed three. The workshop compromised on three days in the office, with the fourth day by agreement with the sector sponsor.

10. Bonus pool
Mateus raised an alternative scheme under which the bonus pool would be set at ten per cent of profit. Discussion showed that few had read the detail, and the decision was deferred until the next workshop. Mateus then withdrew his earlier suggestion that the pool be paid in March instead of December, saying that he had misread the current policy.

11. Board report
The strategy report goes to the advisory board on Wednesday 15 December, and Helena said the date is fixed because the board does not meet again until February. Jasper and Yusuf will draft it together. Helena will review the draft by the end of the month.

12. Client feedback
Helena read an email from a health client, who said Jasper's team had been the easiest group to work with in years. Jasper thanked her. Yusuf mentioned that he was worried about the pace of work in the data team and that two of the juniors seem to be working very late. He did not ask for any action.

13. Any other business
If the data-readiness service is launched in January, Ingrid wondered whether the firm might need a second office. This was noted as a hypothetical and not discussed. Mateus asked who is organising the end-of-year dinner. Nobody knew. Helena asked whether the workshop should have a standing item on the firm's culture, and Jasper said maybe next time.

14. Close
The workshop closed at 4.10 pm. Colleen Tran will be sent a copy of the minutes.
