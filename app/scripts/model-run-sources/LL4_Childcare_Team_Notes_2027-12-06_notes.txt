Little Quokkas Early Learning Centre, team meeting notes
Monday 6 December 2027, 6.30pm in the staff room
Present: Annabel Rourke (director), Esme Fitzgerald, Kwame Boateng, Tahlia Nguyen, Ruth Vasiliadis

Enrolments and the waitlist
Annabel opened by saying that the toddler room has twelve children enrolled and a waitlist of nine, though she wasn't sure the waitlist was accurate because a few families may already have found places elsewhere. Esme asked whether the centre could take a thirteenth child. Annabel said not under the current ratios, and that raising the cap would need approval from the regulator, which she doesn't expect. It was left there.

Staffing
Ruth told the team she will finish at the end of January because she's moving interstate. Annabel said she was sorry to lose her and that it would leave the preschool room short of one qualified educator. Tahlia thought they might be able to cover the gap with a casual, but Annabel said she'd rather advertise for a permanent replacement. She will post the advertisement by the end of this week. Whoever does the first round of phone interviews should be a qualified educator, though no one was named for it. The team agreed that Ruth's handover notes should be done before her last fortnight.

Annabel also said Kwame is moving to the babies' room in February, and that Tahlia will move to the toddler room at the same time. Kwame said he was fine with the move. Tahlia said she was nervous about the change but was happy to go along. Annabel mentioned that Tahlia hasn't worked with under-twos before and she is a little worried how she will cope at first, so Esme will buddy with her for the first month.

Christmas concert
The concert is planned for Thursday 16 December. Esme suggested holding it outside, but Ruth pointed out that if it rains it would have to move inside, and the hall holds only about sixty. Annabel suggested an indoor concert only, with children performing in two sittings to keep numbers down. After some back and forth the team compromised: outdoors with the hall as the wet weather plan, and two sittings regardless. Kwame will check the weather forecast on the Tuesday before the concert and tell everyone. Esme and Tahlia will share the job of making the programme and have it ready by Friday 10 December.

Parent communication
Annabel read out an email from a parent who said that her daughter had loved the new music corner. The team was pleased. Annabel also said that one family has complained, in passing, about the late arrival of the term newsletter, though they haven't made a formal complaint. She asked everyone to try to have room updates in to her three days before the newsletter goes out. All room leaders should send their updates by the deadline.

Photos
Annabel asked whether the team was comfortable with using photographs from the music corner on the centre's social media page, with parents' consent only. Esme, Kwame and Tahlia said yes. Ruth said she'd rather not be photographed herself, which was accepted. Nobody was asked about the new educator who started last week and was not at the meeting, so her view is not known.

Budget
Annabel said the room resources budget would probably be about eight hundred dollars per room, although this could change once the end-of-year figures are in. She'll confirm the amount in the new year. Kwame wondered whether the toys they currently have are even worth keeping. Nobody answered.

AOB
Ruth asked whether the staff room fridge can be cleaned out, since "it's a disgrace". Annabel agreed to ask the cleaners.

Meeting closed at 7.40pm. Notes written by Annabel Rourke and to be circulated to staff on Tuesday.
