Tallowood Brewing Co, fortnightly team meeting
Wednesday 2 February 2028, taproom, 8.30 am

Attendees: Declan Marsh (owner and head brewer), Priti Nair (taproom manager), Ollie Kastellanos (packaging), Hannah Bright (sales), Tomasz Wierzbicki (cellarman)

1. Autumn seasonal release
Two options on the table: a hazy pale ale or a dark lager. Hannah said the bottle shops keep asking for something hazy. Tomasz said the dark lager is cheaper to make and the tanks are free sooner. Vote taken, three for the dark lager, two for the hazy. Dark lager goes ahead for a mid April release. Hannah said she is not thrilled but will sell it.

2. Hops
The hop contract with the Tasmanian grower covers about 60 per cent of next season. Declan said he heard the grower had a poor harvest and might cut allocations, but that is hearsay from another brewer at the trade night. Declan will ring the grower and ask directly. No date set. Tomasz pointed out that if the allocation is cut we only have enough Citra for two brews.

3. Canning line
Ollie reported the canning line was down for two days last week with a seal fault. Only one contractor in Victoria services the machine. Ollie said if that contractor is unavailable we are stuck, and nobody else here knows how to do the full service. Declan said he would pay for Ollie to get trained, if the course is under $2,000. Ollie to find out the cost by Friday.

4. Keg numbers
Priti said we lost 38 kegs last quarter. Later in the meeting Priti corrected this to 28 after checking the returns sheet. Declan said either way it is too many. Priti will email the three pubs with the highest unreturned counts.

5. Taproom trivia night
Priti proposed a monthly trivia night on the first Thursday. Declan said yes in principle. Hannah asked who would host. Nobody offered. Left open. Priti to think about whether a regular customer might do it.

6. Staffing
Tomasz mentioned that Kayla, one of the casual taproom staff, has said she may be leaving in April to move interstate. Priti said she would not count on it until Kayla confirms in writing. Priti worried that Saturday nights will be thin if she goes. Declan asked whether we should start advertising now. Priti said wait.

7. Compliments
Declan passed on a message from the Fitzroy bottle shop that the last pilsner batch was the best they have stocked. Everyone cheered. Declan thanked Tomasz and Ollie for the long weekend of brewing.

8. Cold room
The cold room is making a noise. Someone should get it looked at before the summer peak. No owner was named.

9. Festival booth
Hannah said the Geelong beer festival booth is $1,400. Declan will say yes if the festival can confirm we can pour full strength beer. Hannah to ask them and report back next meeting.

10. Small talk and other
Brief chat about the footy tipping comp and whether Tomasz's mother's Greek biscuits would be at the next meeting. Declan said bring them.

Actions
Declan: ring the hop grower, no date.
Ollie: find out canning service course cost, by Friday 4 February.
Priti: email the three pubs about unreturned kegs, by Monday 7 February.
Priti and Tomasz: count remaining kegs on site and match to the returns sheet, by the end of next week.
Hannah: ask the festival about full strength pouring, before the next meeting.
Nobody: trivia host.
Nobody: cold room noise.

Next meeting Wednesday 16 February, same time.
