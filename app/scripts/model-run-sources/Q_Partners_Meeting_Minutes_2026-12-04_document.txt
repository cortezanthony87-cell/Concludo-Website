Calder and Webb Advisory
Minutes of the partners meeting held on Friday 4 December 2026 at the Richmond office

Present: Alicia Calder (chair), Gregor Webb, Naomi Pillai
Apologies: none
Recording: the chair asked whether the meeting could be recorded for the minutes. Gregor agreed. Naomi asked that it not be recorded, so the recorder was switched off and these minutes were taken by hand.

1. Graduate intake
Gregor proposed taking on two graduates in February. Naomi said the firm cannot supervise two properly while the Kensington audit is running. After discussion the partners resolved to take on one graduate in February and review a second in July. Alicia will brief the university careers office by 18 December.

2. Kensington audit
Naomi reported that the audit fieldwork is on track but that she is the only partner registered to sign the audit opinion, so the sign off depends entirely on her availability in March. Gregor noted the client has twice paid late this year. The partners agreed to ask for a 30 per cent deposit before fieldwork resumes in January. Gregor will send the deposit request to Kensington by Wednesday 9 December.

3. Office lease
Alicia raised whether the firm should buy rather than lease when the lease ends in 2028. No decision was made. Alicia will ask the accountant for a buy versus lease comparison, with no date set.

4. Other business
Gregor said he believes the firm's professional indemnity premium will rise by around 20 per cent but had not seen the renewal notice. Naomi thanked Gregor for the client survey, which she said was the clearest the firm has run. It was asked whether the firm's privacy policy covers the new client portal; nobody present could say, and the question was left for the next meeting.

The next meeting will be held on Friday 5 February 2027 at 10 am.
