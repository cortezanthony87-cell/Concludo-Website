ACTION MINUTES - Waratah Court Owners Corporation, committee meeting
Thursday 10 December 2026, 6.00 pm, held in the building's community room

Present: Lorraine Whitfield (chair), Sanjay Patel (Banksia Strata Management), Elise Montague (treasurer), Brett Vukovic (owner representative, Unit 12).

Insurance renewal
Sanjay reported that the building insurance policy expires at midnight on 31 December. The insurer has confirmed that the date cannot be extended, so the committee must choose a quote by then. Two quotes are on the table. The cheaper one has a higher excess for water damage. Lorraine said she would rather pay more than carry that risk, given the burst pipe two years ago. Elise pointed out that the premium difference is roughly $1,400 a year, though she has not confirmed it with the broker. The committee resolved to accept the higher quote with the lower excess.

Action: Sanjay to confirm acceptance with the insurer in writing by Tuesday 15 December.

Lift servicing
Brett asked whether the lift contractor, Aldous Lifts, holds a current licence. Nobody in the room knew. Later in the meeting Sanjay checked his phone and confirmed that the licence is current until August 2027. Brett was satisfied. The committee agreed to renew the service contract for another twelve months.

Action: Elise to sign the renewal and return it to Aldous Lifts by the end of the month.

Noise complaints (background)
Lorraine mentioned, for background only, that a resident in Unit 4 complained last month about late-night noise from the courtyard. No formal complaint has been lodged and nothing was decided. Brett said he would keep an eye on it and let the committee know if it escalates. No action was taken.

Levy notices
Sanjay reminded the committee that the next levy notices must go out by 21 January to meet the by-law notice period, which cannot move. He asked Elise to approve the budget figures before then. Elise said she would do that after the new year, probably the second week of January.

Action: Elise to approve the budget figures for the levy notices by Friday 8 January.

Overdue levies
Unit 7 is now 60 days behind on levies. Sanjay has sent two reminders. Lorraine asked whether the committee should start recovery action. Brett thought it was premature, since the owner has said by phone that payment is coming. The committee agreed to wait until the end of January and decide then if nothing has arrived.

Garden works
Lorraine raised the question of replacing the front hedge. Nobody wanted to take on getting quotes. Sanjay said he would normally do this but is fully booked. The item was left open.

Positive feedback
Elise thanked Sanjay for the clear summary pack sent before the meeting. She said it was the first time she had understood the sinking fund report.

Next meeting: Thursday 11 February 2027, 6.00 pm.

Meeting closed at 7.20 pm.
