OWNERS CORPORATION PLAN NO. PS 412876
"HARBOUR VIEW APARTMENTS", 18 BAYSIDE PARADE, WILLIAMSTOWN VIC 3016
MINUTES OF THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
Held on Friday 19 December 2026 at 6.30 pm in the building's community room

1. Attendance and quorum
Present: Margaret Oyelaran (Chair), Stephen Tran (Secretary), Elena Markovic (Treasurer), Graham Pike, Joanna Leach, Dev Patel, and Ruth Ellison (Dalton Strata Management, minute taker). Apologies: Bruce Hallett. Nine of the fourteen lot owners were present or represented by proxy. A quorum was declared.

The Chair noted that Ruth Ellison would take the minutes and asked whether everyone consented to their votes being recorded by name. All agreed except Graham Pike, who asked that his votes be recorded as "owner of Lot 9" only. This was accepted.

2. Confirmation of previous minutes
Moved: Joanna Leach. Seconded: Dev Patel. Carried unanimously.

3. Financial report
Elena Markovic reported that the administrative fund is in surplus by approximately $11,000, and the maintenance fund is, in her words, "probably a little short" for next year. Two lot owners owe levies totalling $6,800, overdue since September. It was resolved that Dalton Strata will send final notices by 9 January 2027.

4. Motion 1: Lift replacement reserve
That the maintenance fund levy be raised by 8 per cent. Moved: Elena Markovic. Seconded: Stephen Tran. Votes for: 6. Votes against: 3 (Graham Pike, Joanna Leach, Dev Patel). The motion was carried.

5. Motion 2: Replace the roof membrane in 2027
Moved: Graham Pike. Seconded: Joanna Leach. After discussion about the single quote of $87,000, the Chair suggested a compromise: obtain two further quotes and revisit the question at a special meeting. Graham accepted, and the original motion was withdrawn. Stephen Tran will collect the further quotes by 27 February 2027.

6. Motion 3: Install electric vehicle chargers in the car park
Moved: Dev Patel. Seconded: Joanna Leach. Votes for: 3. Votes against: 6. The motion was lost.

Dev asked, as a hypothetical, whether a smaller trial of two chargers would be accepted. No decision was made, and the matter was deferred to the next AGM.

7. Motion 4: Reverse the earlier decision to close the rooftop garden
At the October committee meeting, it was resolved to close the garden. The meeting now voted to reverse it. Moved: Margaret Oyelaran. Seconded: Dev Patel. Votes for: 7. Votes against: 2. Carried.

8. Risks raised
(a) Ruth noted that the building's insurance expires on 31 January 2027, a fixed date, and the renewal has not yet been requested. Nobody present volunteered to take the lead on this item. It was left unassigned.
(b) Elena observed that she is the only person who can access the bank account. She said she would seriously consider adding a second signatory, but made no commitment.
(c) Margaret said the current building manager is leaving at the end of March.

9. Other business
Joanna asked whether the lifts could be serviced quarterly instead of every six months. Ruth said she would find out and report back to the committee by 30 January 2027.

Stephen asked, rhetorically, "Who would ever vote for another year of this noise?" in reference to the car park gate. No action was taken.

Graham Pike thanked the Chair for running a fair meeting. Ruth said the building is one of the best-run in the area, and Margaret thanked the volunteers.

10. Close
There being no further business, the Chair closed the meeting at 8.10 pm.

Minutes taken by Ruth Ellison, Dalton Strata Management.
