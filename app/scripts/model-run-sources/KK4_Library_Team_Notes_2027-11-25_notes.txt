Tarrawarra Regional Library
Team meeting, Thursday 25 November 2027
Attendees: Ines Castellanos (branch manager), Bill Nakamura, Ruth Adeyemi-Clarke, Oskar Brandt, Tahlia Moloney
Notes by Oskar

Summer reading program
Ines opened the meeting by saying the summer reading program for children has attracted about 120 sign ups so far, though she thinks some of those are double ups from last year's list. She would like Tahlia to take over the registration desk for the program and to have the sign up sheets printed by the second week of December. Tahlia agreed. Ruth said that last year the program ran out of the small stickers in the first week, and asked whether anyone had ordered more. Nobody had. Ines asked Oskar to order them, and he said he would do it on Monday.

New catalogue system
The council has decided that the library network will move to a new catalogue and loans system in March. Ines said the training is two half days, and that the supplier has promised that the old records will transfer cleanly, though she is not going to count on that. Bill said he is a little worried about Ruth, who has used the current system for nineteen years and has said openly that she does not like the idea of changing. Ruth said that was fair, but that she was more worried about the front desk than about herself. Ines said she would sit with Ruth after the first training day and see how it went. Ruth said that would be welcome. Ines will ask the supplier whether a training session can be held in the evening for Tahlia, who has a second job on Wednesdays.

Opening hours over summer
Ines asked the team whether the library should open on the Saturday before Christmas. Bill said that last year only fourteen people came in. Oskar said that if a free event was run, many more might come. Nobody wanted to decide now, so the question was deferred until the next meeting. In the meantime Bill will count the Saturday visitors from last December and bring the number.

Staffing
Ines told the team that Bill has accepted a position at the university library and will finish up at the end of January. She said the team's thanks go to him for fifteen years of work. Bill said he was sorry to leave, and that he would help to hand over the interlibrary loans, which only he knows how to do properly. Ines asked who would take on interlibrary loans after he goes. Ruth said she would be glad to learn it. Oskar said he would be, too, if there was time. It was left open for now. Ines said she would advertise the position in the new year. If the budget is cut, she may not be able to replace him at all.

Community room
The community room was used by the knitting circle, the chess group and a local historical society last month. Tahlia said the historical society would like a display cabinet. Ines asked who would be paying for it. Tahlia did not know. Oskar wondered aloud whether the council would fund it, but Ines said she couldn't see why they would.

Mentions
Ruth said a regular visitor had told her he thought the library's new large print shelf was wonderful. Ines said she would add a thank you to the staff newsletter. Tahlia mentioned that the weekend heat has been difficult in the back room, and that she hopes to get a fan. Ines said it would be fine to buy one under thirty dollars from petty cash.
Oskar said that he had been talking with the neighbouring branch, and the manager there told him she is thinking of leaving too. It is not known to be true, and it was agreed that it should not go any further.

Close
Ines said she'd email these notes to the team and to the area manager on Friday. Nobody had any other business. Meeting closed at about 11:20. Next meeting: Thursday 9 December.
