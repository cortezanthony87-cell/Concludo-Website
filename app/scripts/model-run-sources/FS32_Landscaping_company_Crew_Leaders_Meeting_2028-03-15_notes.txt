Corbett and Nunn Landscapes, crew leaders meeting
Wednesday 15 March 2028, 6.45 am, yard office, Dandenong South

Present: Trevor Corbett (owner), Maria Esposito (office and scheduling), Darren Kelly (crew leader, construction), Aisha Rahman (crew leader, maintenance), Jack Nunn (crew leader, planting), Pete Sorensen (apprentice supervisor)

Notes by Maria.

Before we started Maria asked if she could photograph the whiteboard roster and send it to the nursery so they know the delivery days. Trevor, Aisha, Jack and Pete said yes. Darren said no, he does not want his name or the crew list sent outside the business. Maria agreed to send the nursery only the delivery days and no names.

1. Council tender, Heathmere Reserve
The tender is due Friday 31 March at 5 pm and the council has said no late submissions. Trevor said it is worth about $140,000 if we win it, though he admitted that is his best guess. Maria said the pricing sheet is only half done. Trevor will finish the pricing by Monday 27 March. Aisha will write the maintenance section. Jack and Darren share the planting and paving method statements and will have a draft by Friday 24 March.

2. New ute
We need a second dual cab. Darren wanted the diesel at $62,000, Jack wanted the cheaper petrol at $54,000. Trevor said the quote he read out earlier as $62,000 was wrong, the dealer's figure is $59,500 drive away. After discussion we voted. Diesel won three votes to two with Trevor abstaining. Trevor said he will only sign if the bank confirms the lease rate stays below 7 per cent. If it goes above that, we revisit.

3. Plant supply
Nearly all our stock comes from Mulgrave Nursery. Aisha said that if they have another bad season or put up prices we have no backup. Maria will ask two other nurseries for trade pricing, no date set. Jack said what if we grew some of our own tube stock in the shade house? Trevor said it was an idea to think about after the tender, not now.

4. Weather
Wet forecast for Thursday and Friday. If more than 20 mm falls by Thursday night, the Camberwell paving job moves to next week and the Hawthorn clean up comes forward. Darren said he would believe it when he saw it, the forecast has been wrong four weeks in a row. Heard from a supplier that the bagged mulch price is going up soon, not confirmed.

5. Crew and training
Pete said Lachlan, the first year apprentice, is struggling with the mower service checks and probably needs more time with someone patient. Pete will pair him with Aisha on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Trevor asked that all crew leaders go through the safety folder this month. No one was named. Maria noted that the chemical spray records are missing for two weeks in February, and nobody took responsibility for finding them.

6. Praise and other
Trevor thanked Aisha for the Doncaster clients' feedback, three of them rang to say how tidy the site was. Short chat about the football, and Pete brought in lemon tart from his mother, which went quickly.
