Dear Stephen and Adaeze,

Re: Our meeting on Wednesday 6 October about your refinance and the investment unit

Thank you both for coming in on Wednesday, and for bringing the folder of statements. It made the afternoon much easier. Adaeze, thank you also for the lovely biscuits. I am writing to set down what we talked about and what we agreed, so that we are all working from the same page. Please read it carefully and tell me if I have got anything wrong.

Where things stand. You currently owe about five hundred and ten thousand dollars on the home loan, at a rate of 6.4 percent. You told me you would like to bring the repayments down, and also to free up money for the deposit on a small investment unit. I gave you my view that with the current lender you are probably paying too much, but I should be clear that I cannot promise what any lender will offer until the applications are assessed. The rates I mentioned on the day were indicative only.

You asked whether you could borrow enough to buy the unit without selling the car. I said it is possible but I do not know yet, and I would rather not guess. You also asked, half jokingly, whether the bank would ever say no to a couple with a clean record like yours. I said the answer is that lenders sometimes do, so I would not say never.

What we agreed

1. Refinance. We will apply to refinance the home loan to a new lender, and I will lodge the application once I have the documents listed in item 4. You decided this at the meeting, and Stephen, I noted that you were happy to go ahead while Adaeze wanted to think about it overnight. Adaeze told me on the phone yesterday that she is also happy.

2. Fixed or variable. You were split on this. Stephen preferred to fix the whole loan for three years. Adaeze preferred to stay variable. We agreed on a compromise: fix three hundred thousand dollars of the loan for three years and leave the rest variable. I will put that structure in the application.

3. Offset account. You decided to keep an offset account. The fee is about three hundred and ninety-five dollars a year. The two of you agreed that the saving on interest is worth more than that.

4. Documents. Stephen will send me the last two payslips for each of you, and the most recent tax assessment, by Friday 15 October. Adaeze will send the statements for her savings account and the credit card by the same date. Please send a photo of the card statement only, not the card itself.

5. Credit card. I asked you to reduce the limit on Adaeze's card from twelve thousand dollars to five thousand dollars before the application. Lenders count the whole limit, not just what you owe. Adaeze, you said you would call the bank next week.

6. Investment unit. We discussed a unit in the Doncaster area, with a price of up to six hundred and fifty thousand dollars. I suggested we should not make any decision on this until the refinance has settled. You both agreed to wait. This is deferred, and we will talk again in the new year.

7. Insurance. I recommended that you review your life cover. You said you would look at it, but nobody has taken it on yet and I have not set a date. Please let me know if you would like an introduction to an adviser.

8. Valuation. The new lender will arrange a valuation of the house. I will tell you the date as soon as it is booked. Please make sure the house is tidy that day and that someone can let the valuer in.

9. Gifting. Stephen's father offered to give you forty thousand dollars toward the unit. I said that gifts need a letter confirming it is not a loan. Please do not accept the money, and do not move it into your accounts, before I have checked the letter. This applies to both of you.

10. Time frame. The lender has told me it usually takes about three weeks from a complete application to approval, though this can be quicker or slower. Your current fixed-rate deal does not end until next April, so there is no deadline except the one you set yourselves.

Something I noticed while writing this. You are about to lose the cashback offer from the new lender if the application is not lodged before the end of the month. This is a fixed date, so the documents in item 4 matter.

A few other things. I also raised the question of whether you wish to keep the same lender for the unit, and you thought it was a good question. You asked whether the new lender would let Stephen's brother act as guarantor. I do not know. I will ask. Finally, you asked whether Wattle Grove would ever flood. That is a question for the council, not for me.

Privacy. I made notes during the meeting but did not record it. I did not ask whether you were happy for me to share your details with the valuer's office. Please tell me if you are not. I have not discussed your file with anyone outside my office.

Thank you once more. I thought you both had your finances in very good order. I will write again when the application has been lodged.

Kind regards,

Lachlan Ng
Mortgage broker, Ng Home Loans
