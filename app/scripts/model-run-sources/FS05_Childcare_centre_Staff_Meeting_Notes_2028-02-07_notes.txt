Wattle Grove Early Learning Centre, staff meeting notes
Monday 7 February 2028, staff room, 6.15 pm after close

Attendees: Jodie Ferrante (director), Priyanka Sethi (assistant director), Haruka Mori (kinder room lead), Brendan Oyelade (educator, toddlers), Tom Abernethy (cook)
Not present: Narelle Quigley (new educator, on leave this week)

1. Welcome and small stuff
Jodie thanked everyone for the Friday night working bee. The sandpit looks great. Tom's banana bread at the working bee was declared the best thing anyone has eaten this term. Brendan wants to know if the sausage sizzle is happening again. Nobody answered, so it stays a maybe.

2. Excursion or incursion
Haruka wants to take the kinder group to the Willow Creek farm in April. Priyanka prefers a farm animal incursion at the centre because the bus hire is dear and the ratios are harder off site. Discussion went around twice. Put to a vote: excursion 2, incursion 3. Incursion wins. Haruka said she is disappointed but fine with it. Priyanka to get a quote from the animal handler.

3. Fees and the bus quote
Jodie said the bus hire quote was $620 for the day. Priyanka corrected this later in the meeting, the quote is $680 and the $620 was last year's price. Noted as $680.

4. Staffing
Brendan said he has heard that the centre down the road is paying educators $2 an hour more. Jodie said she can't confirm that and it is only hearsay. Priyanka raised that Haruka has been covering the toddler room most mornings and looks tired. Jodie to have a quiet chat with Haruka this week, nothing formal. Jodie is also worried that Tom will give notice if the kitchen refit keeps being pushed back. Tom did not say much. If he does leave, we have no backup cook, and what would we do then? Left as an open question.

5. Photos and the newsletter
Jodie wants to use photos of the educators for the March newsletter. Everyone at the meeting said yes, they are happy to be in it. Photos of children only where the signed permission form is on file. Narelle starts properly next week and nobody has asked her yet whether she is comfortable with her photo and name going in. Jodie will ask her on Monday before anything is sent to the printer.

6. Safety and compliance
The first aid kits in each room need checking and restocking. Someone needs to do this before the council visit. No owner was named. The allergy poster in the kitchen is also out of date and needs reprinting. Nobody put their hand up for it.

7. Programming for term two
Haruka and Brendan to share the planning of the outdoor learning program, with a draft to Jodie by Friday 25 February. If the grant comes through, we could buy the new shade sails, but that depends on the council result which should be known in late March.

8. Next meeting
Monday 7 March, same time.
