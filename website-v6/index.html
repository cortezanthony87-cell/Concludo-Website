Workflow builder

Type what you want to happen. Concludo builds it.

Most automation tools ask you to learn them first: which trigger, which node, which field maps to which. That is the guesswork, and it is why so many automations never get built. In Concludo you describe the outcome in a sentence, in your own words, and the workflow comes back drawn, labelled and explained in plain English before it has run once.

  • No syntax to learn, and no vocabulary of triggers and nodes to learn first
  • Change it the same way you built it, by saying what should be different
  • Every workflow that reaches outside Concludo carries an approval step that cannot be configured away

Governance

Built so nobody has to take its word for anything

Most of the risk in AI at work is not that the model gets something wrong. It is that nobody can tell when it has. Three rules hold everywhere in Concludo, and they are enforced in the software rather than described in a policy.

Concludo proposes. A person disposes.

No agent sends a message, assigns work or changes a record on its own. Every external action waits for a named person, including the routine ones.

Measure the work, never the people

Concludo does not calculate, store or display an individual score, ranking or productivity index. The database schema refuses the column, so it cannot be added by accident.

Every claim points at a record

Each output names what it was drawn from, what it assumed, and what it could not find. An answer with nothing behind it says so instead of filling the gap.

Questions

The things people ask first

Does Concludo record or join my meetings?
No. Concludo does not record, join or transcribe anything. You capture the conversation however you already do, on an AI pen, a phone, or a meeting platform, and you paste the transcript in. Nothing new sits in the room, and nothing new appears in the attendee list.
Which recorders and platforms does it work with?
Any of them. If you can get text out of it, Concludo can work with it. That is a deliberate design choice rather than a limitation: tying the method to one device would make the device the point, and the device is not the point. Concludo is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any device maker, meeting platform or note-taking service.
What happens to a transcript after I paste it in?
It is treated as protected information. You control retention and deletion, and the record shows what was read, what was produced from it and who approved the result. Transcripts are never used to rank, score or assess an individual, and there is no code path in the product that would produce such a figure.
Will it send things on my behalf?
Not unless you approve that specific thing. Drafts, record changes, calendar invitations and briefing releases all wait in the Approvals Centre. Nothing in the product is permitted to mark itself as authorised to send.
Can I buy it today?
No. The Workspace is built and partly working, and it is not yet billable or open to customers. There is no checkout on this site. The five free tools are live and need no account, and the prices on the pricing page are published as indicative so that buyers can tell us where they are wrong. Concludo Pty Ltd is a registered Australian company, ACN 701 605 898, and what you see here is what exists rather than what is planned.
Is a language model writing this?
Not today. Everything the Workspace produces now is rule-based extraction from what you pasted, and no language model is called anywhere in the product. That is why every line can be traced back to the sentence it came from. When a language model is live, tested and covered by the contract terms, this site will say so plainly rather than quietly changing the wording.
What is not built yet?
Branded PowerPoint export, automatic follow-up email drafting, single sign-on and usage limit enforcement. The CRM and the Communication Hub run on demonstration data. Those things appear in the plan designs on the pricing page because that is where they will sit, and they are not claimed as working anywhere on this site.

Melbourne, Australia. Concludo Pty Ltd, ACN 701 605 898. Australian English, Australian time zones, and an Australian business you can email directly.
