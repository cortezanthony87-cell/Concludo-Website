The Workspace

Everything that happens after somebody stops talking

One place that takes a transcript and produces the work, holds the customer record, sorts the inbox, runs the routines, and makes a person approve anything that leaves the building.

Try the five free tools
Copilot and the home screen

Start from a paste, not a blank page

Create a project, paste the transcript, and the outputs are drafted against it. Ask the Copilot what was decided, what is overdue and who owns it, and the answer arrives with the records attached. Where the records do not answer, it says so rather than inventing a paragraph.

  • Transcript archive and saved projects
  • Decision memory across sessions, not only within one
  • Outputs exported as documents, slides or structured data
Email Command Centre

Mail sorted by what it is asking of you

Work and personal accounts are isolated and never combined, and one is shown at a time. Threads are grouped by what they need rather than when they arrived: needs attention, reply queue, actions and deadlines, waiting on somebody else, quick wins and the backlog.

  • Suspicious threads held aside with the reasoning written out, advisory only
  • Reply drafts carry no Concludo attribution or footer
  • Every dismissal is written to the audit record
CRM and KAM Copilot

The account record, kept honest

Pipeline, leads, commitments, organisations, people and account plans. The KAM Copilot reads one account through six lenses and separates what is recorded from what it inferred from what nobody has answered, then gives you the question that would close each gap.

  • Proposed record changes queue for confirmation
  • The buying centre is shown as roles that are filled or empty, never as a judgement about a person
  • Promises on both sides, with the date each one was given
Agents and approvals

Nine agents, one door

Agents draft; the Approvals Centre is where a person decides. Outgoing replies, external invitations, record changes and document releases all pass through the same queue, and the audit record keeps what was read, what was produced and who cleared it.

See the nine agents
Concludo Calendar

The day, with the commitments in it

Home, day and twenty four hour, week, month, agenda and a task board. The meetings sit beside what those meetings created: actions with dates, call backs somebody promised, deadlines a conversation set, and the approvals waiting on you. Day notes stay with the day.

  • Every item is clickable back to the record it came from
  • Australian time zones, with no second diary to keep in step
  • A date reaches your real calendar only after you approve it

Build state, 20 October 2026

What works today, and what does not

Everything on this site describes software that exists. This is the part most product pages leave out, so it is written down here instead.

Working

Transcript to summary, action plan and decision log. Projects, decision memory, the action tracker, search and insight statistics. Team workspaces, roles, approvals, the audit log, retention and legal hold. Provider sign-in for mail and calendar. All of it rule-based: no language model is called anywhere.

Partial

The Email Command Centre and the Copilot, rule-based, with sending held behind approval. Workflows: storage is real, most execution is simulated. PDF reports produce real files. The free trial and the billing screens exist but are not yet enforced server side.

Not built

Branded PowerPoint export and automatic follow-up email drafting. Single sign-on, usage limits and seat enforcement. The Enterprise plan. The CRM and the Communication Hub run on demonstration data only. Privacy, terms and refund pages are in legal review.

The rule this site follows. Say what the Workspace does today. Do not call anything AI-powered until a language model is live, tested and covered by the contract terms. Name a recorder or a platform only with the independence disclaimer. Every product screen on this site is demonstration data.
