1. What Notewright is

Notewright is a meeting-transformation company. It helps professionals
and businesses run fewer, better, more valuable meetings, and it gives
them the software to turn every important conversation into finished
work: summaries, action items, follow-up emails, decision logs, plans
and branded presentations, from any transcript, captured on any device.

The teaching attacks the problem that sits in front of transcription:
meetings with no clear purpose, too many attendees, no decision record,
weak delegation and no follow-up. Notewright teaches when not to meet,
the questions every meeting must answer, how every action gets an owner
and a date, and how to use AI note-takers responsibly. The Workspace
then does the conversion in minutes.

  Positioning statement: for meeting-heavy professionals and teams,
  Notewright is the meeting-transformation education and workspace
  platform that helps people run fewer, better meetings and turn
  conversations into action. Unlike device apps, meeting bots, generic
  AI tools, courses or consultants, it combines the Meeting Mastery
  Workbook, practical software outputs, certification, workshops and
  corporate implementation into one device-agnostic system.

2. Offer overview

The product stack has five layers sold as one ladder: the workbook, the
education platform, the Workspace, the certification loop, and the
content engine that turns the curriculum into market education.
Customers enter for free and climb as far as their pain justifies.

3. Free diagnostic tools

Five free tools, extracted from the workbook worksheets. Their job is
diagnosis and trust: they name the pain, capture the email address, and
move people onto the ladder.

-   Meeting Cost Calculator: puts a dollar figure on a team’s meeting
    load from their own numbers.

-   The Ten Questions card: the questions every meeting must answer
    before it deserves to exist.

-   Meeting Audit Scorecard: scores a real meeting against the standard.

-   AI Note-Taker Setup Checklist: responsible, consent-forward setup
    for any capture device.

-   Delegation Script Pack: word-for-word scripts that give every action
    an owner and a date.

6. The Notewright Workspace: technical specification

Built, version 2. The Workspace is the software layer of the ladder:
paste any transcript, leave with finished work. It is capture-agnostic
by design; paste is the universal integration, and capture-side
connectors are deliberately excluded because they would reposition
Notewright as a meeting bot competing with the platform vendors’ bundled
AI. [Certain: standing product decision]

Figure 2. The paste-first Workspace flow, from any capture source to

Outputs

From one pasted transcript: Summary; Action items with owner and date
enforced on every line; Follow-up email; Decision log; Action plan;
Business plan draft; Workflow charts rendered as real flowcharts; and a
one-click branded PowerPoint compiled from the generated notes. Every
output supports copy and JSON export.

Architecture, as built

-   Front end: a single static web application containing the eight
    course modules, the Workspace, the template and prompt libraries,
    and plan-gated access matching the subscription tiers.

-   Backend: an Express server exposing POST /api/generate (transcript
    to output), POST /api/projects and GET /api/projects/:id, and acting
    as the AI proxy so the API key exists only server-side, never in the
    browser. [Certain: standing build rule]

-   Hosting and source: Render for the app and backend; GitHub as the
    source of truth.

-   Deck export: PowerPoint generation via PptxGenJS, branded to the
    customer’s outputs.

-   Integrations: output-side only. The first connector, post Gate 4, is
    Zapier or Make; compatibility guides, not native connectors, cover
    the meeting platforms.

Current limitations, stated honestly

-   The sign-in screen is a prototype gate only: it stores and secures
    nothing, and a real auth provider (Supabase, Auth0, Firebase or
    Clerk) must be connected before real users. [Certain]

-   Data is session-only in the browser today; saved projects, history
    and the searchable archive require the hosted backend and a
    database. [Certain]

-   Some AI-heavy flows may run within Notewright usage limits or on the
    customer’s own AI subscription; this pricing decision is still to be
    locked in. [Assumption]

8. Live delivery: workshops and audits

Meeting Reset Workshop, AU$500 to 2,500

A live workshop for small teams and meeting-heavy departments: a meeting
audit, the Ten Questions, delegation training, a live
transcript-to-output demonstration, and an implementation sprint. The
delivery kit: pre-work survey, cost calculator, live audit, delegation
exercise, transcript demo, implementation checklist and follow-up pack.
Every workshop books its own follow-up before it ends.

Meeting Waste Audit, AU$1,500 to 3,000, credited toward any program

A two-week paid diagnostic: calendar-load analysis; three observed
meetings scored against the Ten Questions; a staff pulse survey; and a
cost model built from the client’s own meeting counts, attendee numbers,
durations and blended hourly cost, concluding with a recommended Meeting
Standard. Return on investment is always computed from the client’s own
data, never asserted from industry averages.

9. The corporate transformation program, AU$10,000 to 50,000

Priced by team size, depth and certification scope, with part-payment
upfront. The deliverable stack:

-   Meeting Waste Audit (2 weeks): the diagnostic above, run first and
    credited into the program.

-   Meeting Standard design: norms, templates, the decision-log system,
    and the AI note-taker and consent policy tailored to the
    organisation.

-   Training: two half-day workshops plus train-the-trainer so the
    standard survives the engagement.

-   60-day implementation: Workspace rollout, weekly check-ins, adoption
    support.

-   Measurement report: before-and-after meeting hours, decision latency
    and follow-up rate, in the client’s numbers, executive-ready.

Payback logic: the audit quantifies current meeting cost from client
data, and the program needs to recover only a stated fraction of that
measured cost to pay back inside a quarter; the audit output states that
fraction explicitly for each client. [Certain as arithmetic]

10. Certification, AU$199 to 999

Three tracks: Notewright Certified Meeting Operator; Notewright
Certified AI Note-Taker; and the Notewright Team Meeting Standard.
Certification runs as a beta first and is assessed on practical outputs:
an agenda rewrite, a meeting audit, a delegation plan, a decision log, a
consent script and a follow-up pack. [Assumption: beta pricing, tested
with the first cohort]

Positioned honestly as a Notewright practical standard, never as formal
accreditation. Commercial launch is gated behind delivered workshops and
corporate programs with documented outcomes (Gate 6), so the badge is
worth displaying before it is sold at scale.

13. What is not included

-   The device or hardware. Notewright is device-agnostic education and
    software; customers bring their own recorder. Device independence is
    strategic and consent guidance ships in every package.

-   Legal advice. Consent and privacy guidance is general and practical,
    not legal counsel.

-   Formal accreditation. Certification is a Notewright practical
    standard, assessed on real outputs.

Guarantee: a 14-day free trial on Starter and Pro lowers the risk of
subscribing.

14. Readiness and sell sequence

What is ready now: Starter’s static value (course modules, templates,
prompts and guides) plus the built Workspace v2 and its backend, scoped
for deployment. What waits on build: hosted app, real authentication,
persistence, seats, admin and SSO. [Certain: current build state]

The sell sequence follows the gates: validate demand and price with
Starter first; launch Pro once the hosted app, backend and a real auth
provider are live; open Team once seats, admin and SSO exist. Workshops,
audits and the corporate program are sold through the founder motion
from Month 4 under Gates 5 and 6. Nothing is published or sold under the
Notewright name until Gate 1 clears, and every price in this document is
confirmed with real buyers at Gate 2 first.
